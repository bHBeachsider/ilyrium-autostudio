# Characters — Reminiscences

## old_man
- **Key:** `old_man`
- **Age:** 80s
- **Build:** heavyset, fleshy jowls, thick neck
- **Hair:** balding with wisps of combed-over hair; sparse stubble
- **Face:** deep forehead creases, drooping nearly-shut eyes, bulbous wrinkled nose
- **Wardrobe:** rumpled open-collared button-down shirt
- **Affect:** wheezy, warm, meandering; chuckles at every memory regardless of how crude or horrifying it is
- **Role:** sole character and narrator. A direct-to-camera monologuist whose fond reminiscences — a friend urinating in a priest's soup, belly-button golf-ball tricks at dance halls, a coworker scalped by a fan blade — get darker while his cozy delivery never changes. The whole strip is his talking head under a 'Reminiscences' masthead.
