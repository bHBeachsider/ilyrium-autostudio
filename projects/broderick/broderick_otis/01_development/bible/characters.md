# Characters — Otis the Hound Dog

## otis
- **Key:** `otis`
- **Physical:** Floppy-eared hound dog; white body with black patches on ears and tail, big black nose, goofy wide eyes, long lolling pink tongue, simple collar. Affect: boundless wacky cheer — until the unprovoked lunge, when his eyes narrow and his jaws sprout jagged fangs; back to oblivious contentment on the vet table.
- **Role:** Title character. The neighborhood's favorite pet, celebrated by the theme-song narration, who one day lunges at little Billy's throat and is put down.

## billy
- **Key:** `billy`
- **Physical:** Small skinny boy around 8 years old, short hair, sometimes a backwards baseball cap, t-shirt and shorts, freckles. Affect: pure delighted kid-joy, then raw terror.
- **Role:** "Little Billy," Otis's adoring neighbor kid and the victim of the lunge that ends everything.

## vet
- **Key:** `vet`
- **Physical:** Slim middle-aged man, receding hairline, scrub top, stethoscope around his neck. Affect: calm, neutral, professionally detached.
- **Role:** The veterinarian who administers the final injection in the last panel.
