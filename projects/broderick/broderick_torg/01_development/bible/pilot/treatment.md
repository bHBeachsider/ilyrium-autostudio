# Pilot Treatment — Torg the Defrosted Neanderthal

**Logline:** A scientifically defrosted Neanderthal — demonstrably intelligent by stone-age standards — is drilled on the concept of social media fourteen hours a day by a relentlessly cheerful institute instructor, and after three beats of slack-jawed silence produces his entire verdict: "KAAA... BAAAA..."

**Treatment:** In a white institute media room furnished with nothing but a monitor, a bright-eyed instructor points at a mock social feed and patiently explains "likes" to Torg — a hulking, bare-chested Neanderthal gripping his stone-tipped spear — comparing thumbs-up icons to things he might genuinely like, "much like a fire, or fresh meat from a hunt." She presses on through funny photos, comments, and videos that make us laugh; Torg squints. A full beat of dead silence follows, the composition unchanged, her finger still on the screen. Finally Torg responds: two earnest guttural syllables. A mock-documentary narration rushes to his defense — berries, herbs, and a mouse skull in his stomach prove he was a discerning eater and a social creature by necessity — but social media is another matter, and the institute is on it, fourteen hours a day. END plate over Torg's shoulder.
