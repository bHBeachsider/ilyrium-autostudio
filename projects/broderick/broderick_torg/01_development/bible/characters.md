# Characters — Torg the Defrosted Neanderthal

## torg
- **Key:** `torg`
- **Physical:** Massive, hulking adult male Neanderthal; bare-chested with slabs of muscle and a thick neck; heavy protruding brow; broad flat nose; jutting lower jaw with crooked teeth; scraggly beard and mustache; wild matted hair. Carries a tall wooden spear with a leaf-shaped knapped stone point at all times.
- **Wardrobe:** None visible above the waist; shaggy fur garment implied at his shoulder line.
- **Affect:** Squinting, slack-jawed incomprehension; earnest but mute, finally producing only guttural gibberish syllables.
- **Role:** Title character. A scientifically defrosted Neanderthal — demonstrably intelligent in survival terms — being drilled on social media by the institute 14 hours a day.

## instructor_woman
- **Key:** `instructor_woman`
- **Physical:** Slender adult woman; long straight hair past her shoulders; bright upturned smile; small pointed nose.
- **Wardrobe:** Plain long-sleeved top.
- **Affect:** Relentlessly cheerful, patient, over-enunciating teacher energy that never flags despite zero feedback.
- **Role:** Institute instructor who points at a monitor and explains likes, funny photos, comments, and videos to Torg.
