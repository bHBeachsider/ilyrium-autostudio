# Characters — Memory Lane (Where Are They Now?)

A yearbook-style anthology: each panel profiles one former high-school archetype, narrated by
deadpan caption boxes that reveal their unremarkable adult careers. No character appears in more
than one panel.

## gary
- **Key:** `gary`
- **Physical:** Teenage boy, about 16, lean, dark mussed hair, heavy brow, lopsided smirk, t-shirt and wristband, twisted sideways at his school desk mid-performance.
- **Affect:** Mugging, attention-hungry class clown.
- **Role:** Panel 1 subject. "Fancied himself quite the class fool"; now a loading dock associate at a major chemical company.

## classmate_gawker
- **Key:** `classmate_gawker`
- **Physical:** Scrawny teenage boy, buck teeth, bulging delighted eyes, leaning over the desk behind Gary.
- **Affect:** Easily entertained audience.
- **Role:** Background witness who validates Gary's clowning.

## classmate_girl
- **Key:** `classmate_girl`
- **Physical:** Teenage girl, shoulder-length hair with bangs, seated at her desk writing, eyes down.
- **Affect:** Unimpressed, diligent.
- **Role:** Background counterweight — the one student ignoring Gary.

## doreen
- **Key:** `doreen`
- **Physical:** Teenage girl, about 17, curvy build, dark voluminous wavy hair, heavy-lidded eyes, large glossy lips, hoop earrings and bracelets, cropped midriff-baring top and short dark skirt, arms crossed, chin raised.
- **Affect:** Self-assured, soaking in attention.
- **Role:** Panel 2 subject. "Captured the hearts of many, including some of the shop teachers"; now a communications associate for a car dealership.

## admiring_student
- **Key:** `admiring_student`
- **Physical:** Scrawny student with long straight hair, slack jaw, tongue lolling, clutching books while shuffling past.
- **Affect:** Helplessly smitten.
- **Role:** Background witness to Doreen's effect on the school.

## flapjacks
- **Key:** `flapjacks`
- **Physical:** Teenage jock, about 17, thick-necked and broad, face and neck covered in acne, gap-toothed ecstatic grin, short side-parted hair, dark letterman sweater with a large letter D, palming a football overhead.
- **Affect:** Triumphant, swaggering.
- **Role:** Panel 3 subject. Real name Dick Jackson; year-round six-sport jock and bully; now an associate development director at a major pet food company.
