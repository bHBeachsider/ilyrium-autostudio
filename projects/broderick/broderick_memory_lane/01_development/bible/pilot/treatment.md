# Pilot / episode treatment — Memory Lane (Where Are They Now?)

**Logline:** A deadpan yearbook retrospective checks in on three high-school legends —
the class clown, the heartbreaker, and the six-sport bully — and finds them all
working middle-management jobs nobody dreams about.

**Treatment:** Under an elegant script banner — "Where Are They Now?" — a flat
narrator flips through three black-and-white yearbook memories. Gary, the class fool,
twists sideways at his desk mid-bit, a buck-toothed admirer howling behind him while
a girl keeps writing, unimpressed; the caption lands his fate: loading dock associate
at a major chemical company. Doreen, arms crossed and chin raised, captures the hearts
of many — including some of the shop teachers — as a smitten student shuffles past
with his tongue out; she is currently a communications associate for a car dealership.
Finally "Flapjacks" (real name Dick Jackson), acne-blazed and gap-toothed, palms a
football overhead in his letterman sweater while the narrator recites his six-sport,
year-round jock-and-bully resume — and his present role as associate development
director at a major pet food company. The vertical END bar drops with no moral
offered: the past was loud, the present is beige, and nobody escaped.
