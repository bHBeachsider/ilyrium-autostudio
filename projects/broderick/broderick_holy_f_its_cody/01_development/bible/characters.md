# Characters — Holy F* It's Cody

## cody
- **Key:** `cody`
- **Age:** mid-20s
- **Build:** scrawny, narrow shoulders
- **Hair:** messy spiky dark hair with long sideburns; sparse chin stubble
- **Wardrobe:** white-framed sunglasses worn at all times, dark band t-shirt
- **Affect:** smug, breezy stoner; goes from grinning and chatty to fully catatonic — slack-jawed, drooling, motionless — after smoking
- **Role:** the agent of chaos. Front-seat passenger who lights a joint in his friend's Uber, hotboxes the car, and spends the rest of the strip zonked while the fallout unfolds around him.

## uber_driver
- **Key:** `uber_driver`
- **Age:** 40s
- **Build:** lean
- **Hair:** chin-length dark bob with bangs
- **Face:** large nose, heavy-lidded deadpan eyes
- **Wardrobe:** sleeveless top
- **Affect:** weary, unflappable, customer-service serene; never raises her voice
- **Role:** Cody's friend and new Uber driver. Begs Cody to be cool, fails, then absorbs the businessman's meltdown with infinite deadpan, answering every escalation with 'REMAIN CALM SIR.'

## businessman
- **Key:** `businessman`
- **Age:** 40s
- **Build:** average, squared shoulders
- **Hair:** short dark hair, sharp heavy brows
- **Wardrobe:** dark suit, white shirt, striped tie
- **Affect:** entitled, volcanic temper; escalates from impatience to full tirade, then collapses into stammering panic
- **Role:** the pick-up passenger wedged into the middle seat of a hotboxed car. Rages about the slow ride and threatens a bad rating until the secondhand smoke gives him a contact high and a panic attack — the strip's karmic punchline.

## hippie_mascot
- **Key:** `hippie_mascot`
- **Age:** indeterminate, weathered
- **Build:** head only (mascot bust)
- **Hair:** long stringy hair under a headband; bushy beard and mustache
- **Wardrobe:** huge round dark sunglasses
- **Affect:** beatific, grinning
- **Role:** non-narrative mascot for the closing mail-order gag page advertising 'more stale-ass pot comedy' for $9.99 cash.
