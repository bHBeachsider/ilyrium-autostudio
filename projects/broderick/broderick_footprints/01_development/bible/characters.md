# Characters — Footprints on the Beach

This two-panel strip is a parody of the devotional "Footprints in the Sand" poem.
No character is clearly drawn on-panel; both presences exist through narration and
traces left in the sand.

## dreamer
- **Key:** `dreamer`
- **Physical:** Never shown directly; present only as the narrating voice and as one set of footprints (later a winding trail with a dragging groove) in the sand.
- **Wardrobe:** N/A (off-screen narrator).
- **Affect:** Earnest, wounded, questioning; recites the poem sincerely.
- **Role:** Narrator of the dream. Walks the beach of his life with the Lord and asks why, at his lowest moments, only one set of footprints remains.

## the_lord
- **Key:** `the_lord`
- **Physical:** Never shown in person; implied by the second set of footprints in panel one and by their conspicuous absence — alongside towering ladder-like structures climbing out of frame — in panel two.
- **Wardrobe:** N/A (unseen presence).
- **Affect:** Silent; the wordless second panel serves as the entire answer.
- **Role:** The addressee of the poem's question. The strip's punchline replaces the comforting "I carried you" answer with a deadpan visual of where He actually went.
