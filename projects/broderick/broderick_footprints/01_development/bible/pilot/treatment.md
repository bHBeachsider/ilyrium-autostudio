# Pilot Treatment — Footprints on the Beach

**Logline:** A reverent recitation of the "Footprints in the Sand" poem asks where the Lord went during life's saddest stretches — and the wordless answer is that He was down the beach playing volleyball.

**Treatment:** Over a wide dream-beach at the edge of a flat gray sea, the classic devotional poem unfolds in hushed calligraphic earnest: two sets of footprints recede across the sand as the narrator's life flashes by, and the question lands with genuine hurt — "Where were you, Lord, when I needed you most?" The reply is total silence and a cut: same shoreline, no comfort, no carrying. A long-haired, bearded figure in a pale robe rises at a beach volleyball net, mid-spike, locked in a friendly game with two swimsuited players under a dash-stroked sun, utterly absorbed and entirely unbothered. No dialogue, no apology — just the squeak of sand, a ball in the air, and a hand-scrawled END in the corner.
