# Pilot / episode treatment

**Logline:** A wry autobiographical memoir of 7th-grade industrial arts under "Josh Schpiegler" — a sweating, bellowing metals teacher who mocks two kids as gossipy hens and finally keeps the narrator off honor roll over a botched metal pad holder.

**Treatment:** Under a mock-grand title card — ornate green script, "No.707B," and an ascent-of-man silhouette that ends in a slouching man chugging a bottle — the narrator recalls 7th grade, when industrial arts electives delivered him into the hands of metals teacher Josh Schpiegler (fake name; "any resemblance to the real metals teacher is purely coincidental. Just kidding he was an asshole"). Schpiegler is introduced mid-bellow, finger thrust at the door, daring lazy students to leave. Rumor said ol' Josh got in a little hot water down in the Deep South — pictured deadpan in a damning hooded flashback — and fled north in a pickup with a "USA - LOVE IT OR GET THE HELL OUT!" bumper sticker, past smokestacks into the Gawden State. Stuck with him a second semester, the narrator and his benchmate endure the "CLUCK CLUCK CLUCK — gossipy old hens!" tirade with faint smirks. The finale: project day, a crooked metal pad holder, a bloodied fingertip rendered in the strip's only splash of red, and Schpiegler's ghostly glower presiding over the verdict — a "C," no honor roll. END. A deadpan memoir where the injury and the insult arrive in the same panel.
