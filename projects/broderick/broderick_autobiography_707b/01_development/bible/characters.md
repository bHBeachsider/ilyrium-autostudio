# Characters — Snapshots of Life No.707B (Autobiography)

## schpiegler
- **Age:** 50s
- **Build:** Heavyset, thick neck, slabby shoulders
- **Hair:** Bald on top with a full graying beard; thick black rectangular glasses; perpetual sweat droplets
- **Wardrobe:** Gray button-up work shirt with a pen and pencil in the breast pocket; in the rumor flashback, a white pointed full-head hood while driving; flees north in a pickup truck with a "USA - LOVE IT OR GET THE HELL OUT!" bumper sticker
- **Affect:** Permanently enraged hard-ass; bellowed ultimatums and mocking impressions; hates everyone, especially the narrator
- **Role:** Antagonist. The 7th-grade metals (and graphic arts) teacher — introduced under a fake name ("Josh Schpiegler," also rendered "Jack Spiegler") — who torments the narrator and ultimately keeps him off honor roll with a "C".

## narrator
- **Age:** 12–13 (7th grade)
- **Build:** Skinny
- **Hair:** Long straight hair past the shoulders; pointed nose
- **Wardrobe:** Hand-lettered JETHRO TULL t-shirt in class; plain crew-neck sweater on project day
- **Affect:** Faint smirk under fire; dismay at his own bleeding finger; wry retrospective voice in the captions
- **Role:** Protagonist and autobiographical voice. Gets stuck with Schpiegler two semesters running, botches the metal pad holder project, bleeds for it, and loses honor roll.

## classmate
- **Age:** 12–13
- **Build:** Slight
- **Hair:** Dark rounded bob
- **Wardrobe:** Plain light shirt
- **Affect:** Wary frown, keeps his hands flat on the bench
- **Role:** The kid who sat next to the narrator; co-target of Schpiegler's "gossipy old hens" tirade.
