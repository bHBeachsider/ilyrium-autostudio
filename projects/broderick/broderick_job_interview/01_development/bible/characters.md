# Characters — Job Interview

## interviewer
- **Key:** `interviewer`
- **Physical:** Heavyset balding man, middle-aged, scruffy gray-flecked beard, bow tie, parked behind a big desk with a laptop. Affect swings from genial distraction to volcanic, spittle-flying rage without warning.
- **Role:** The interviewer at XJHK Industries Incorporated. Conducts a normal interview for two questions, then rechristens Jason "Pervy-Peeping-Tom-the-Pervert" and explodes when Jason dares ask a question back.

## jason
- **Key:** `jason`
- **Physical:** Thin man in his late 20s, neatly combed hair, rectangular glasses, slim suit and tie, perched on a swivel office chair with hands folded. Affect: polite, eager, desperately agreeable — even while being abused.
- **Role:** Job candidate Jason Andrews. Delivers flawless corporate buzzwords and absorbs escalating insanity in hopes of landing the job.

## host_man
- **Key:** `host_man`
- **Physical:** Muscular man in his 30s, thick curly dark hair, broad shoulders, tight t-shirt, huge grin with a winking eye. Affect: manic salesman charisma.
- **Role:** Infomercial host of the epilogue, recapping "what we learned" and pushing the predatory permanent subscription.

## host_woman
- **Key:** `host_woman`
- **Physical:** Woman in her 30s, blunt blonde bob, enormous fixed smile, leaning into frame at the host's shoulder. Affect: unblinking advertising cheer.
- **Role:** Co-host of the infomercial epilogue, silent partner in the subscription pitch.
