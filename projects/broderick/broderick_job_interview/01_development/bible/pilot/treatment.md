# Pilot Treatment — Job Interview

**Logline:** A desperately agreeable job candidate absorbs an interview that swerves from buzzword ritual to deranged abuse — then an infomercial duo sells the lesson back to us as a permanent subscription.

**Treatment:** Behind a big clean desk at XJHK Industries Incorporated, a heavyset bow-tied interviewer types at his laptop without looking up while thin, polite Jason Andrews answers the rote questions perfectly — name, then a flawless string of results-driven, needle-moving buzzwords. Then the interview turns: breezy as a calendar item, the interviewer announces he'll be calling Jason "Pervy-Peeping-Tom-the-Pervert" from now on. Jason, undeterred: "Does that mean I have the job?" Wrong move. The interviewer erupts across the desk, finger jabbing, spittle flying, screaming threats while Jason sits frozen, hair blown straight back. Hard cut to a grinning infomercial duo — muscular winking host, blonde co-host with a fixed smile — recapping "what we learned" beside a button offering a permanent $299.99/month subscription that, per the fine print, transfers to your descendants in perpetuity. ~30 seconds, scratchy grayscale gag-cartoon throughout.
