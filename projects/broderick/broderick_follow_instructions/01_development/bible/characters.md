# Characters — Follow Instructions

## passerby
- **Key:** `passerby`
- **Physical:** Smooth featureless instruction-manual humanoid; rounded bald head, soft pudgy build, no clothes or facial detail beyond a dot eye and simple mouth. Breezy, self-satisfied affect — strolls in whistling, helps without being asked, then turns smug.
- **Role:** The "helpful" stranger who assembles the box for the struggling figure, then insults him with a pictogram dunce cap. Loses his head for it — literally.

## manual_reader
- **Key:** `manual_reader`
- **Physical:** Smooth featureless instruction-manual humanoid; slightly taller rounded build, bald head, perpetual frown. Clutches an open fold-out instruction booklet to his chest in every panel. Affect runs from frustrated confusion to volcanic rage.
- **Role:** The struggling assembler. Asks for help with a wordless question mark, gets shown up and called a dunce, and answers by swatting the passerby's head clean off.
