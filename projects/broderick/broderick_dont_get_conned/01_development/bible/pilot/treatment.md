# Pilot Treatment — Don't Get Conned (Fonzi Scheme)

**Logline:** A schlubby mark asks the one question that should kill the scam — "risk-free?" — and a greaser con man closes the deal with nothing but a double thumbs-up and one word.

## Treatment
A micro-short built from a single held scene on a blank white void, staged like a PSA that immediately fails at its job. Under the title "FONZI SCHEME," a doughy, balding man in a too-tight white tee spreads his hands and walks himself through the pitch he wants to believe: hand over $1,000, get $5,000 back — today — risk-free? Across from him, filling the frame with pompadoured, leather-jacketed, 1950s-cool confidence, the con man delivers the entire prospectus: both thumbs up, toothy grin, "CORRECTOMUNDO!" That's it — that's the due diligence. The film holds the beat a moment too long, letting the audience watch the mark accept a thumbs-up as a financial instrument, then cuts out. The comedy is the void: no office, no paperwork, no product — just charisma and a pun, which is exactly how the real thing works.
