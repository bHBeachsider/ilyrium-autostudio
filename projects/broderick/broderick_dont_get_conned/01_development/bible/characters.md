# Characters — Don't Get Conned (Fonzi Scheme)

## fonzi_conman
- **Key:** `fonzi_conman`
- **Age:** 40s
- **Build:** tall, broad-shouldered, confident stance
- **Hair:** black pompadour, slicked back; square jaw, big toothy grin
- **Wardrobe:** dark leather jacket with knit collar and cuffs over a light shirt, faded jeans
- **Affect:** retro greaser cool — answers everything with double thumbs-up and catchphrases like 'CORRECTOMUNDO!'
- **Role:** the con man of the title pun: he runs a 'Fonzi scheme,' promising 5x risk-free returns with nothing behind them but charisma.

## mark
- **Key:** `mark`
- **Age:** 50s
- **Build:** doughy, hunched, sagging belly
- **Hair:** balding with a fringe of dark hair; heavy-lidded, slack expression
- **Wardrobe:** tight white t-shirt, dark trousers
- **Affect:** hesitant but hopeful; wants to believe
- **Role:** the victim. Asks the one question that should end the scam ('risk-free?') and is satisfied by a thumbs-up.
