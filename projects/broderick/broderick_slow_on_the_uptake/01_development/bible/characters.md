# Characters — Slow on the Uptake

## clerk
- **Age:** 40s-50s
- **Build:** Short, soft, hunched; oversized egg-shaped head
- **Hair:** Bald with a single small tuft at the front
- **Wardrobe:** White dress shirt, loose black tie, dark slacks, sneakers; flat cap and vest in the final panel
- **Affect:** Vacant, earnest, perpetually sweating; mangles words and never doubts himself
- **Role:** Protagonist. The dim office errand-runner who hears 'affidavit' as 'Afro David' and brings back a stranger instead of a document, getting himself fired.

## boss
- **Age:** 50s
- **Build:** Lean, angular
- **Hair:** Balding with messy side hair; long sharp nose
- **Wardrobe:** Dress shirt and striped tie, behind an office desk
- **Affect:** Impatient, abusive, explosive; snaps fingers at subordinates
- **Role:** Antagonist. The tyrannical boss who sends the clerk to the courthouse and fires him on his return.

## afro_david
- **Age:** 30s-40s
- **Build:** Slim
- **Hair:** Enormous round afro; rectangular glasses
- **Wardrobe:** Plain t-shirt, light trousers; carries a small handbag
- **Affect:** Flat, wary, increasingly baffled
- **Role:** The innocent stranger named David whom the clerk drags back to the office as the 'affidavit'.

## thin_passerby
- **Age:** 30s
- **Build:** Very tall and thin
- **Hair:** Short dark hair
- **Wardrobe:** Plain gray t-shirt, light trousers
- **Affect:** Baffled
- **Role:** First stranger questioned outside the courthouse.

## suit_passerby
- **Age:** 40s
- **Build:** Average
- **Hair:** Dark, neat; heavy brows
- **Wardrobe:** Light suit
- **Affect:** Irritated, dismissive
- **Role:** Second stranger questioned outside the courthouse; tells the clerk to buzz off.

## unemployment_clerk
- **Age:** 30s-40s
- **Build:** Slim, seated
- **Hair:** Dark, shoulder-length
- **Wardrobe:** Office attire behind a small desk
- **Affect:** Bored, procedural
- **Role:** Unemployment office attendant who calls 'Next!' in the closing gag.
