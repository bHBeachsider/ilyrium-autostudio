# Pilot Treatment — Slow on the Uptake

**Logline:** A dim, sweating office errand-runner is sent to fetch an affidavit from the courthouse, mishears it as "Afro David," and returns with a baffled stranger — getting himself fired into a closing gag he also mishears.

**Treatment:** An abusive boss snaps his fingers at the office's dimmest clerk — a short, egg-headed man in a loose black tie who sweats in discrete flying beads — and barks two orders: the coffee was late, and the AFFIDAVIT must be picked up from the courthouse, ASAP, chop chop. The word disintegrates the moment the clerk leaves the room: "AFTER DOVE IT... AFFER DIVIT... AFFER DAVID..." By the time he's marching past sketched office towers it has hardened into a name: AFRO DAVID. On the courthouse steps he conducts his search with unshakeable earnestness — "ARE YOU DAVID?" — bouncing off a baffled beanpole and a suit who tells him to buzz off, until a slim man with an enormous round afro, glasses, and a small handbag answers "YES. WHY?" Mission accomplished. Back at the office the clerk proudly presents Afro-David from courthouse; the boss detonates, fires him on the spot, and accuses the pair of being a god-damn comedy duo. At the unemployment office, the attendant calls "NEXT!" — and the clerk, now in a flat cap, begins mangling that word too. END. Deadpan grayscale doodle world; the comedy is the clerk's total confidence inside total error.
