# Characters — Holy S*** It's Cody

## cody
- **Age:** Late teens–20s
- **Build:** Scrawny, lanky; hairy armpits when shirtless
- **Hair:** Stringy shoulder-length dark hair; enormous drooping nose; heavy-lidded eyes
- **Wardrobe:** Folded paper fast-food service hat, sleeveless work top and shorts at the grill; shirtless with a waist wrap at the rock show
- **Affect:** Dopey, slack, easily stoked; phonetic metalhead drawl ("TEWTOLLY STEWKED", "RAWK SHEW")
- **Role:** Protagonist. Golden Fry grill cook who sneaks out to the Gods of Metal show and burns the restaurant down.

## boss_man
- **Age:** Middle-aged
- **Build:** Angular, hunched, sharp-elbowed
- **Hair:** Hidden under a folded paper service hat; sharp beak-like nose; permanent scowl
- **Wardrobe:** Paper hat, work shirt/apron; habitually clutching drink cups
- **Affect:** Volcanic temper; mission-statement corporate ranting laced with profanity
- **Role:** Antagonist. Golden Fry manager who denies Cody's half day and ends the strip screaming outside his burning restaurant.

## friend
- **Age:** Teens
- **Build:** Short, stocky
- **Hair:** Pale bowl haircut hanging over his eyes
- **Wardrobe:** Black band t-shirt, baggy shorts, heavy black boots, studded wristband
- **Affect:** Pumped, conspiratorial, monosyllabic stoke ("DUDE!")
- **Role:** Instigator. Talks Cody into sneaking out for the rock show; witnesses the fiery consequences.
