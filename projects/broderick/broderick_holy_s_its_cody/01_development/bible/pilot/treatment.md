# Pilot / episode treatment

**Logline:** A dopey metalhead grill cook sneaks out of his fast-food shift for the Gods of Metal show and comes back to find the restaurant burned to the ground.

**Treatment:** At the Golden Fry grill, droopy-nosed slacker Cody asks Boss Man for a half day — the Gods of Metal rock show is today and he is "TEWTOLLY STEWKED." The boss detonates: no notice, customers' hunger doesn't take half days, the mission is hot delicious fresh food, and Cody's already on thin ice. Sulking over smoldering patties, Cody is tempted by his bowl-cut friend at the door: sneak out on your lunch break, just an hour of RAWK. Resistance lasts four seconds. The two sprint from the storefront in jubilant silhouette, headbang shirtless in a sea of bobbing heads, and stagger out three hours later — at which point Cody dimly recalls he left some burgers on the grill n' whatnot. They return to find Golden Fry erupting in two towering columns of black smoke, the tiny figure of Boss Man rigid at the door screaming "FOCK!" while the boys watch from the foreground in silhouette. "WHOA DUDE." END. Played as deadpan doodle slapstick: total catastrophe, zero remorse, maximum stoke.
