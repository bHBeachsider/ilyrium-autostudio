# Characters — Big Hair

## big_hair_man
- **Key:** `big_hair_man`
- **Physical:** Gaunt middle-aged man, thin build, long droopy nose, heavy-lidded weary eyes. Defining feature: an enormous tangled mass of abnormally thick, rope-like hair strands ("big noodles") piled on his head — a medical condition called Big Hair. Wears a rumpled suit, shirt and loose tie. Affect: exhausted, long-suffering patience with a hard limit.
- **Role:** Protagonist. Rejected at a job interview because of his hair, mocked and prodded at a bar, until he smashes his beer mug into his tormentor's face. Rule: hands off the big hair.

## interviewer
- **Key:** `interviewer`
- **Physical:** Balding lanky man in glasses, middle-aged, office shirt, works behind a laptop at a large desk with a city-skyline window. Affect: bland corporate politeness.
- **Role:** Job interviewer who rejects the protagonist with HR euphemisms about his follicles not being "an optimal fit at this juncture."

## bar_loudmouth
- **Key:** `bar_loudmouth`
- **Physical:** Stocky man in his 30s, spiky short hair, big nose, gap teeth, baggy t-shirt, beer glass perpetually in hand. Affect: loud, braying, zero boundaries.
- **Role:** Bar pest who mocks the big hair, announces it to the room, then touches it — and takes a beer mug to the face.
