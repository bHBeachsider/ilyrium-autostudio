# Characters — Karate Kicks

## husband
- **Key:** `husband`
- **Physical:** Man in his 30s, lean athletic build, short neat side-parted dark hair, light suit jacket with dark tie. Affable, breezy affect at the table; in action panels he is rendered as a pure black silhouette in a martial-arts stance, leg fully extended in high roundhouse kicks.
- **Role:** Protagonist with a karate-kicking compulsion he swears the webinars cured. Snaps at dinner and roundhouse-kicks everyone in the restaurant.

## wife
- **Key:** `wife`
- **Physical:** Woman in her 30s, slim build, short curled dark hair, sleeveless dark dress with ruffled collar. Warm but perpetually wary affect; appears as a seated foreground silhouette during the rampage.
- **Role:** Long-suffering spouse. Extracts the no-kicking promise in panel 1 and screams "HONEY! STOP!" through the entire rampage.

## mr_johnson
- **Key:** `mr_johnson`
- **Physical:** Man in his 40s, broad-faced, stocky build, slicked-back hair, suit with patterned tie and pocket square. Boisterous, back-slapping affect, big laugh, points when he talks.
- **Role:** Dinner companion whose good-natured golf ribbing triggers the husband. First kick victim.

## mrs_johnson
- **Key:** `mrs_johnson`
- **Physical:** Woman in her 30s, slim build, wavy light hair (dark updo and buttoned skirt suit in the kick panel), elegant blouse, pleasant sociable affect.
- **Role:** Mr. Johnson's wife. Polite dinner companion; third kick victim.

## waiter
- **Key:** `waiter`
- **Physical:** Man in his 20s, slim build, dark hair, vest and bow tie over white shirt, carrying a domed serving tray. Professional cheer turned to shock.
- **Role:** Innocent bystander; second kick victim mid-service.
