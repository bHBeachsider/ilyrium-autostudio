# Pilot / episode treatment — Results

**Logline:** Two deadpan before-and-after advertisements deliver their results: a diet
that changes nothing, and a facelift that should never have worked this well.

**Treatment:** A mock-infomercial short in stark black-and-white, played in two
clinical diptychs divided by a hard black bar. First, a middle-aged man in polka-dot
boxers stands in exact side profile on a pedestal labeled BEFORE — balding tuft,
glasses, mustache, scrawny limbs, an enormous hairy potbelly. Cut to AFTER: the same
man, same pose, same belly, now merely riding a little higher and rounder. Nothing has
changed; the panel holds anyway, proud of itself. Second, a haggard older woman drags
on a cigarette under her BEFORE label, cheeks and eye pouches sagging in long folds.
Her AFTER arrives: every inch of facial skin yanked drum-tight upward and cinched into
a bunched topknot like a tied sack, eyebrows hoisted into permanent surprise, a
strained smile carved in place — the same cigarette still smoldering in the same
raised hand. No narrator editorializes; the labels say BEFORE and AFTER, the images
say everything else, and the film ends with the results on display.
