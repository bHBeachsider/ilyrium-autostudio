# Characters — Results

A two-gag strip of mock before-and-after advertisements. Each subject appears in exactly two
panels (BEFORE and AFTER) and must read as the same person in both.

## dieting_man
- **Key:** `dieting_man`
- **Physical:** Middle-aged man, 50s, balding with a short tuft of hair on top, rectangular glasses on a large hooked nose, bushy mustache, scrawny arms and legs, enormous round hairy potbelly, sparse chest hair. Wears only black polka-dot boxer shorts. Always shown in full side profile standing on a low labeled pedestal.
- **Affect:** Blank, neutral, uncomplaining.
- **Role:** Subject of the first before/after gag. His AFTER is indistinguishable from his BEFORE — the belly merely sits slightly higher and rounder. The joke is zero results.

## facelift_woman
- **Key:** `facelift_woman`
- **Physical:** Older woman, 60s. BEFORE: limp combed-down grey hair, deeply sagging cheeks and eye pouches, drooping nose, thin downturned lips, drop earrings in stretched lobes. AFTER: facial skin pulled drum-tight upward with strain lines down the neck, eyebrows hoisted into permanent surprise, fixed strained smile, all excess skin gathered into a bunched topknot atop her head. In both: dark high-collar top with a white label card on the chest, one hand raised holding a smoldering cigarette with long dark nails.
- **Affect:** BEFORE: weary and deflated. AFTER: startled, frozen, grotesquely "improved".
- **Role:** Subject of the second before/after gag — a facelift whose results are more alarming than the original face. The unchanged cigarette is the continuity anchor.
