# Characters — Cool Daddy Smooth

## cool_daddy_smooth
- **Age:** 30s–40s
- **Build:** Lanky, narrow-shouldered
- **Hair:** Long straight black hair to the chest; small soul-patch mouth; always behind oversized white-framed sunglasses
- **Wardrobe:** Tall crinkled cone-shaped hat (never removed), black leather jacket over layered necklaces, striped bell-bottom trousers, heeled boots; at the gig, a polka-dot clown suit with a scalloped ruffle collar lettered FLAPPY and giant floppy shoes — hat and shades kept on
- **Affect:** Unshakeable unearned swagger; bored hostility at actual work; profanity around children; revisionist bravado afterward
- **Role:** Protagonist. Cons his way into a party-clown job as "Flappy," ruins the party, gets thrown out, and retells it as him quitting because the job wasn't cool enough.

## interviewer
- **Age:** 30s–40s
- **Build:** Slim
- **Hair:** Tousled dark hair; long pointed nose
- **Wardrobe:** White shirt, dark tie; behind an office desk with a computer
- **Affect:** Pleasant, credulous, routine
- **Role:** Hires Cool Daddy Smooth on the strength of pure bluster.

## party_kids
- **Age:** Toddler to ~8
- **Build:** Small, sketchy and round
- **Hair:** Varied — a grinning short-haired kid, a pigtailed girl with her tongue out, a worried boy with a side-swept fringe, a placid toddler
- **Wardrobe:** Plain t-shirts and play clothes
- **Affect:** Blank staring that flips to mass wailing
- **Role:** The audience; their tears end the gig.

## mother
- **Age:** 30s–40s
- **Build:** Tall, sharp-featured
- **Hair:** Swept blonde bob; earring, beaded necklace
- **Wardrobe:** Cardigan over a dark skirt, flats
- **Affect:** Instant maximum fury
- **Role:** Throws Cool Daddy Smooth out and threatens to call the police.

## punk_friend
- **Age:** 20s
- **Build:** Stocky
- **Hair:** Tall black mohawk on a shaved head with a scrawled circled symbol on the scalp; heavy underbite
- **Wardrobe:** Black leather jacket, chain necklace with pendant, skinny black trousers, boots
- **Affect:** Eager, credulous audience for tall tales
- **Role:** Hears the face-saving version of the story in the alley.

## burger_flipper (standalone gag panel)
- **Age:** Middle-aged
- **Build:** Heavyset, round belly
- **Hair:** Bald with a fringe; hairy legs
- **Wardrobe:** Pale t-shirt, baggy cargo shorts, red sneakers
- **Affect:** Triumphant exertion
- **Role:** Literally flips a burgher in the one-off pun panel "Flipping Burghers."

## burgher (standalone gag panel)
- **Age:** Older
- **Build:** Dignified, robed
- **Hair:** Gray beard under a flat dark cap
- **Wardrobe:** Black doublet, long flowing red robe, black hose and shoes, long wooden staff
- **Affect:** Stoic even while airborne
- **Role:** The flipped burgher; the pun made flesh.
