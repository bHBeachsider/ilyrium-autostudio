# Pilot / episode treatment

**Logline:** A swaggering con man bluffs his way into a party-clown gig, traumatizes a children's birthday party, gets thrown out under threat of police — and retells it to his friend as the day he quit a job that wasn't cool enough for him.

**Treatment:** Cool Daddy Smooth struts in under his never-removed cone hat and oversized white sunglasses — a man composed entirely of unearned confidence. Asked at a job interview whether he has party-clown experience, he fires back that it's literally his nickname ("P.C., PARTY CLOWN, LAUGH IT UP CLOWN! LET'S MAKE IT HAPPEN CHIEF!") and gets hired on pure bluster. At the par-tay, stuffed into a polka-dot clown suit lettered FLAPPY (hat and shades still on), he slurs his way through a magic trick — "PRESTO CHANGE-O... AND ALL THAT SHIT" — until the cheap wand flops over, flowers dangling, and he loudly blames the equipment. The kids erupt into mass wailing; a furious mother storms in pointing at the door and threatening police. Later, in a back alley, he recounts the saga to his credulous mohawked friend, fists clenched in triumph: "I SAID 'THIS JOB AIN'T COOL ENOUGH FOR COOL DADDY SMOOTH!'" END. A coda gag panel — "Flipping Burghers," a man literally flipping a robed Renaissance burgher, complete with reader-grading checklist — closes the film on a deadpan fourth-wall shrug.
