# Pilot Treatment — The Bio-Dome Brigade (Part 1)

**Logline:** When a glib, faceless fixer seals a crew of certified idiots inside a billion-dollar biodome "to test how intuitive it is," he hypnotizes the project's outraged founder into leaping out a high-rise window — then has to pun his way through the board's murder inquiry while his hand-picked morons destroy the dome from the inside.

**Treatment:** A grand establishing shot of the gleaming X5000 biodome in a mountain valley gives way to a stark white control-center corridor, where Dr. Spanker — walrus mustache trembling with rage — confronts a man who is nothing but a black silhouette over the unauthorized crew sealed inside: the crew broke the comms system in five minutes and can no longer be reached. As Spanker's rant peaks, the silhouette calmly hypnotizes him and, on the count of three, sends him hurtling through a high-rise window in a shower of glass. Inside the dome, the brain trust insults the beef-a-roni, throws it at the wall, celebrates that it sticks, and stares slack-jawed at a wall of warnings over the dead vegetation sector none of them remembers from orientation. The fallout lands in a silent white boardroom: a granite-faced chairman demands answers about Spanker's "tragic downfall," the silhouette answers with a tasteless pun and a nervous nyuk-nyuk, an excruciating beat of stillness follows — and the chairman, despite everything, nyuk-nyuks back. END, printed across the silhouette's shoulder.
