# Characters — The Bio-Dome Brigade (Part 1)

## silhouette_man
- **Key:** `silhouette_man`
- **Physical:** Middle-aged, stocky build with rounded shoulders and a slight paunch; always rendered as a featureless solid black silhouette with a prominent hooked nose and a few stray flyaway hairs on top.
- **Wardrobe:** Indistinct (silhouette only); reads as a rumpled suit-and-collar shape.
- **Affect:** Slippery, glib, self-preserving; deflects blame with spin, hypnosis, and bad jokes; gulps when cornered.
- **Role:** Protagonist/anti-hero. The unauthorized mastermind who sealed low-IQ residents into the X5000 biodome, hypnotizes Dr. Spanker into leaping from a window, and stonewalls the board's interrogation with puns.

## dr_spanker
- **Key:** `dr_spanker`
- **Physical:** Middle-aged, wiry build; balding crown with dark side hair; enormous thick black walrus mustache; sharp angry brows.
- **Wardrobe:** Gray two-piece suit, white shirt, dark necktie, white cuffs.
- **Affect:** Indignant, sputtering, morally outraged; melts into slack serenity under hypnosis.
- **Role:** Father of the X5000 biodome. Confronts the silhouette man over the unauthorized crew, gets hypnotized, and is commanded to hurl himself out a high-rise window — his "tragic downfall."

## board_chairman
- **Key:** `board_chairman`
- **Physical:** Heavyset, bull-necked older man; flat-top brush-cut hair; thick black mustache; heavy bags under hooded eyes; large folded hands.
- **Wardrobe:** White dress shirt, wide black necktie, seated in a dark executive chair.
- **Affect:** Grim, immovable, prosecutorial — until he finally cracks a reluctant chuckle at the punchline.
- **Role:** Leads the board's interrogation of the silhouette man after Dr. Spanker's death.

## resident_grunt
- **Key:** `resident_grunt`
- **Physical:** Squat, thick-necked and ape-like; heavy brow, stubbled jowls, smashed nose, short messy hair.
- **Wardrobe:** Plain short-sleeved tee.
- **Affect:** Loud, crude, easily delighted; zero impulse control.
- **Role:** Biodome resident. Insults the beef-a-roni, throws his bowl at the dome wall, and celebrates that it sticks.

## resident_lanky
- **Key:** `resident_lanky`
- **Physical:** Tall, gaunt, long pointed nose, spiky receding hair with stray sprouting strands; lolling tongue when laughing.
- **Wardrobe:** Plain shirt, sometimes a dark work apron.
- **Affect:** Sour onlooker who flips into hysterical laughter; aimless and shameless.
- **Role:** Biodome resident. Audience for the food-throwing, admits he skipped orientation to masturbate, puzzles over the dead plants.

## resident_bald
- **Key:** `resident_bald`
- **Physical:** Short, round bald head, squashed worried face, soft doughy build.
- **Wardrobe:** Plain shirt.
- **Affect:** Resigned, forgetful, food-motivated.
- **Role:** Biodome resident. Forgot everything from orientation; wonders if the dead vegetation is edible.
