# Pilot Treatment — Rock Critics

**Logline:** An aging punk-scene gatekeeper lectures a captive listener that Darby Crash will prove more important than the Beatles, works himself into a profane frenzy over one legendary gig, and ends up alone at a table chanting "that was the show" until the word loses all meaning.

**Treatment:** In a dim room, a scrawny, frizzy-haired punk lifer holds forth at a mute black-silhouetted listener, ranking Darby Crash above the Beatles and reducing scene politics to deranged arithmetic — Iggy Pop minus the Dead Boys... no, divided by the Dead Boys. The lecture combusts: straddling his couch, cigarette trailing smoke, he howls that the Dead Boys' gig at the Pyramid changed Western civilization and gave Reagan a big "fuck you," the boast collapsing into a chain of pure profanity. Then the room is empty except for him, hunched at a table with a drink, insisting that you had to be at that show or you're a poser — that a lot of people claim they were at that show but they were not at that show, and he knows because he was at that show — "that was the show" looping, tightening, disintegrating into a glassy-eyed mantra of the single word "show," repeated into semantic dust. END.
