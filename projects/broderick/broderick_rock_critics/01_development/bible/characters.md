# Characters — Rock Critics

## rock_critic
- **Key:** `rock_critic`
- **Physical:** Scrawny middle-aged man; frizzy, unkempt, thinning hair; sunken stubbled cheeks; wide manic eyes; wiry gesturing hands.
- **Wardrobe:** Black band/club tee with a faded light logo (punk-club style); otherwise nondescript.
- **Affect:** Pompous, obsessive, gatekeeping; speaks in absolute pronouncements about punk history; escalates from pseudo-academic theorizing to profane ranting to a glassy-eyed verbal loop.
- **Role:** The strip's subject and engine. An aging punk-scene know-it-all who ranks Darby Crash above the Beatles, mythologizes a Dead Boys gig, and melts down insisting "you had to be at that show" until the word "show" loses all meaning.

## silhouette_listener
- **Key:** `silhouette_listener`
- **Physical:** Head and shoulder of a stocky figure rendered as a featureless solid black silhouette, seen mostly from behind in the foreground.
- **Wardrobe:** Indistinct (silhouette only).
- **Affect:** Mute, captive audience; gives the critic someone to lecture at.
- **Role:** Foreground framing device in the opening panel; the trapped listener absorbing the critic's monologue.
