# Characters — Le Monde Extérieur

## porch_smoker
- **Key:** `porch_smoker`
- **Physical:** Elderly man, 70s, heavyset with rounded shoulders. Bald scalp with a few sparse wiry strands of hair sticking up. Wears a dark suit jacket. Almost always seen from behind, seated in a high-backed porch chair, cigarette in his right hand, occasionally a drinking glass in his left.
- **Affect:** Motionless, deadpan, endlessly patient. Smokes and stares.
- **Role:** The observer. He watches the street from his porch for the entire strip, speaks only twice — once to despair that he no longer understands anything, and once to flatly predict the street man's death. He is the audience's surrogate for the incomprehensible outside world.

## street_man
- **Key:** `street_man`
- **Physical:** Wiry middle-aged man, thin build, spiky thinning hair, hooked nose, manic grin. Wears a plain long-sleeve crew-neck shirt, dark belt, and slacks.
- **Affect:** Manic, gleeful, utterly oblivious. Alternates between two moves: a deep lunge with both fists raised, and a smug hip-wiggling stroll with hands clasped behind his back.
- **Role:** The incomprehensible spectacle. He paces and dances back and forth across the street for no discernible reason, ignoring honking cars and near-death encounters.

## car_driver
- **Key:** `car_driver`
- **Physical:** Adult man, slicked dark hair, sharp snarling features. Seen leaning out the window of a sedan with his fist raised.
- **Affect:** Explosive road rage.
- **Role:** First vehicle encounter — screams at the street man ("GET OUT OF ROAD ASS-CLOWN! WANT TO GET RUN OVER??"), then screeches off leaving tire marks.

## truck_driver
- **Key:** `truck_driver`
- **Physical:** Burly man with a heavy beard and thick arms, glaring from the high cab window of a large truck.
- **Affect:** Silent, looming menace.
- **Role:** Final vehicle encounter — bears down on the oblivious street man, horn blasting, as the strip ends on the porch smoker's prediction of death.
