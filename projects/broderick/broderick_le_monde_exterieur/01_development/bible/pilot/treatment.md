# Pilot / episode treatment — Le Monde Extérieur

**Logline:** From his porch, an old man watches a stranger dance joyfully in traffic
and concludes — flatly — that he understands nothing about the world, and that the
silly son-of-a-bitch is gonna die.

**Treatment:** A single locked shot for the whole film: over the shoulder of a
heavyset old man smoking in a high-backed porch chair, gazing at a quiet gray
suburban street. A wiry, spiky-haired man appears and begins an inexplicable ritual —
a deep fists-raised lunge, then a smug hands-behind-back hip-wiggle stroll — back and
forth across the road, over and over, while the smoker never moves and the smoke
curls. A sedan nearly flattens him; the driver screams "GET OUT OF ROAD ASS-CLOWN!"
and screeches off, leaving looping tire marks the dancer ignores. In the lull the old
man finally speaks, despairing that he no longer understands anything — the meanings
behind the concepts behind the purpose. The dance resumes. A massive truck looms in,
horn blasting, the dancer strolling obliviously into its path, and the old man
delivers his deadpan prophecy as the END card drops: "THIS SILLY SON-OF-A-BITCH IS
GONNA DIE."
