# Characters — Stock Art Beards

## bro_1
- **Key:** `bro_1`
- **Age:** early 30s in scene 1, 50s in scene 2, 70s in scene 3
- **Build:** lean, becoming gaunt and frail with age
- **Hair:** slicked-back hair with a waxed handlebar mustache and long pointed beard; later grey-streaked and receding; finally bald with wisps of white hair and a thin white chin beard
- **Wardrobe:** grey t-shirt (30s), suit and striped tie (50s), baggy light jacket (70s)
- **Affect:** preening and competitive ('I aged better than you'), souring into bitter resignation
- **Role:** the survivor. Proposes nothing but outlasts everyone, keeping the 20-year pact alone until the brewery is a Chipotle and the verdict is 'this was a shit idea anyhow.'

## bro_2_pete
- **Key:** `bro_2_pete`
- **Age:** early 30s (only appears in scene 1)
- **Build:** round-faced, soft
- **Hair:** full bushy beard, light tousled hair
- **Wardrobe:** light knit sweater
- **Affect:** the enthusiastic instigator — 'totes' energy
- **Role:** Pete. Proposes the every-20-years pact, then is absent at the first reunion; reported dead secondhand ('HE DIED I THINK. SOMEONE TOLD ME THAT.').

## bro_3
- **Key:** `bro_3`
- **Age:** early 30s in scene 1, 50s in scene 2
- **Build:** solid, growing heavy and slumped
- **Hair:** dark groomed full beard and swept-back hair; later bald with a drooping thin mustache and long chin goatee
- **Wardrobe:** black t-shirt with chain necklace (30s), grey t-shirt (50s)
- **Affect:** mellow agreeable wingman, aging into vacant detachment
- **Role:** the go-along friend. Shows up at the 20-year mark to deliver Pete's death notice with total indifference; absent (presumed dead) by the 40-year mark.
