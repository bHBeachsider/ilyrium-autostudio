# Style Analysis — Stock Art Beards (SEED v0, 2026-06-11)

Panels examined: all 3 in `panels/` (the complete strip), landscape ~4:3.

## Medium determination
**Hand-drawn digital, grayscale, photo-referenced figure style.** Evidence: figures are drawn with stock-illustration realism — accurate proportions, fine directional hatching in the beards, modeled cheekbones and brows — consistent with the strip's own title joke (the bros look like stock art). The environment is the opposite register: skyline and wall rendered as faint, loose, light-gray scrawl, and signage hand-scrawled in wobbly outline capitals ("ARTISINAL BREWERY", misspelling included; "CHIPOTLE" in panel 3). Flat hard-edged gray fills on garments, no gradients, freehand stroke texture throughout — digital drawing, not AI render and not photography.

## Line / color / shading / lettering
- **Line:** controlled, realistic contour for figures; fine hatch strokes for beards, hair, and facial modeling. Environment lines are loose, faint, fast.
- **Color:** none. White paper, black ink, two or three flat grays.
- **Shading:** flat garment fills (mid gray, dark gray) plus hatch modeling on faces; no washes, no gradients.
- **Lettering:** typeset all-caps comic font, balloon-less, floating in white space above/beside each speaker; time-skip captions ("20 YEARS LATER...") top-left in larger weight; bold "END" bottom-right of the final panel. Signage is hand-scrawled outline lettering — deliberately the crudest mark in the strip.

## Composition and framing habits
- Fixed location across all three panels: same wall, same faint skyline upper-left, signage upper-right — only the sign text, the cast count, and the men's ages change.
- Figures bust-to-waist height, anchored to the bottom edge; heads in the middle band; the top third belongs to caption + signage.
- Cast shrinks 3 → 2 → 1 with the empty space deliberately widening around the last man; panel 3 pushes him small into the lower-left against white void.
- Dialogue reading order = left-to-right speaker order; no pointer lines needed.

## Continuity risks
1. bro_1 ages across three eras but must keep identity anchors: the pointed beard silhouette (dark → grey-streaked → thin white) and lean build.
2. The wall/skyline scrawl should be a locked reusable plate; redrawn backgrounds will read as a location change.
3. Signage misspelling "ARTISINAL" is canon — spell-correcting it breaks the joke.
4. Figure realism must never tip into photoreal or slide into cartoon; the stock-art register is the premise.
5. Empty space in panel 3 is content — generators will want to fill it.

## Keep vs fix for regeneration
**Keep:** fidelity clash (rendered figures vs ghost-scrawl world); grayscale flats + hatching; fixed camera spot across eras; hand-scrawled outline signage including the misspelling; shrinking cast and widening void; balloon-less comic-font dialogue; "END" stamp.
**Fix:** gray values drift between panels (suit vs tee fills); panel 1's three dialogue blocks crowd each other; skyline scrawl inconsistent panel-to-panel (should be one plate); beard hatch density varies enough to read as different artists at small sizes.
