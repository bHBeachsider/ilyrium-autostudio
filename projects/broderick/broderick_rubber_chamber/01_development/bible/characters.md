# Characters — Into The Rubber Chamber Area That Leads To The Toilet

## yabo
- **Age:** Middle-aged (late 40s–50s)
- **Build:** Heavy-set, thick neck, paunchy torso
- **Hair:** Bald with light stubble on the scalp; large protruding ears; heavy jowls and a bulbous nose
- **Wardrobe:** Dark crew-neck t-shirt with ribbed collar; dark track pants with light side stripes
- **Affect:** Permanently enraged; hair-trigger temper; glares, snarls, and bellows; channels all frustration into one signature act of violence
- **Role:** Protagonist. Life confounds him; anyone who knocks on his door gets thrown "into the rubber chamber area that leads to the toilet."

## census_taker
- **Age:** Adult (30s–40s)
- **Build:** Slender, lanky
- **Hair:** Short, light, flies loose while bouncing
- **Wardrobe:** Pale button-up shirt, dark belt, gray trousers, white socks, dark shoes
- **Affect:** Meek and polite at the door; flailing ragdoll terror once airborne
- **Role:** First victim. Knocks to ask for a moment of Yabo's time; gets hurled through the rubber chamber and flushed headfirst down the toilet.

- **Hair:** Unknown
- **Wardrobe:** Unknown
