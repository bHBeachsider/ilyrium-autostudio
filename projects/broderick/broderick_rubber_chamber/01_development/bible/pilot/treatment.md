# Pilot / episode treatment

**Logline:** A permanently enraged bald man answers every knock at his door the only way he knows how — by hurling the visitor into the rubber chamber area that leads to the toilet.

**Treatment:** Against a stark white void, a title card introduces Yabo — middle-aged, heavy-set, bald, and furious at existence itself. Narration warns us: life confounds Yabo, and if you don't watch out he'll throw you "into the rubber chamber area that leads to the toilet." Three knocks at his front door detonate him instantly ("WHO THE HELL IS IT!"). A meek census taker barely gets through his opening pleasantry before Yabo seizes him and launches him bodily through the air, roaring his catchphrase. The hapless victim ricochets in ragdoll arcs around a springy white rubber chamber — BOING, BOING, BOING — before being flushed headfirst down a giant toilet, legs kicking from the bowl. Yabo barely has time to exhale before three more knocks sound: a meter reader, flat and oblivious, announces himself from behind the door. Yabo's rage reloads. TO BE CONTINUED. The film plays as deadpan slapstick — explosive cartoon violence delivered with clinical, absurdist flatness, the cycle primed to repeat forever.
