# Style Analysis — The Cruuuuzing Dood (SEED v0, 2026-06-11)

Panels sampled: 01 (title card), 02 (rap-chant close-up, dialogue-heavy), 04 (cliff finale/END). 3 of 4 total panels.

## Medium Determination
**Hand-drawn digital with soft brush rendering; limited flat color.** Evidence:
- The driver's face carries soft digital-brush tonal shading (cheek/jaw modeling, smoke wisps) — more rendered than the grayscale Broderick strips, but contours are loose hand ink, not AI-smooth or photoreal.
- The sedan is drawn with wobbly hand contours over flat blue fills with simple chrome highlights — clearly illustrated.
- Title card is typeset: oversized slanted bold-italic display font, mechanically clean — digital type, not hand lettering.
- Backgrounds are pure white void or flat poster-like silhouette bands (forest, cliff) — graphic, not environmental rendering.

## Line / Color / Shading / Lettering
- **Line:** Loose, confident, variable contour; sketchier than the grayscale strips (hair spikes, hand/fingers). Motion streaks and debris flecks for the airborne car.
- **Color:** Limited and assignment-based — each element owns one flat hue (blue car, red polo, green forest, gray cliff, tan skin). No ambient color, no sky color.
- **Shading:** Soft digital-brush modeling on face and hands; flat planes elsewhere; darker flat shadow shapes on cliff and forest bands. No cast shadows on the white void.
- **Lettering:** Dialogue is bold all-caps stacked column-left in white space, no balloon outlines. Title is slanted italic display type. "END" is bold white italic over the forest band.

## Composition & Framing Habits
- One subject per frame, floated in white void; zero set dressing inside the car (just wheel and driver).
- Repeated medium close-up from front passenger side, eye level, for both driving beats — static cam, change is in the face.
- Finale jumps to a wide low-angle exterior; graphic poster composition with flat depth bands.
- Text occupies the empty half of the frame opposite the figure.
- Double-vision ghosting (offset translucent duplicate head) used as an intoxication device.

## Continuity Risks
1. Eyes must stay heavy-lidded/nearly shut — any generator default to alert open eyes breaks the character.
2. Gold-tinged stained teeth: models will "fix" them to white; restate explicitly.
3. The sedan is a boxy 1970s four-door in slate blue — silhouette must match between title, interior, and cliff shots.
4. Cigarette between fingers on the wheel is a locked prop in every driving shot.
5. Soft-shaded face vs flat-fill car: a generator may unify the rendering level; preserve the split.
6. White void interior: do not let renders invent a windshield, dashboard, or scenery.

## Keep vs Fix (for regeneration)
**Keep:**
- White-void minimalism; one-hue-per-element flat color assignment.
- Soft brush face shading over loose ink contour.
- Heavy-lidded stoned squint, gold teeth, red polo, cigarette prop.
- Poster-flat finale with layered green bands and motion streaks.
- Stacked all-caps balloon-less dialogue.

**Fix:**
- Title-card typeface and hand-drawn art feel disconnected — consider hand-lettering the title or filtering type to match line wobble.
- Rendering level varies (face highly modeled, car nearly flat) — lock a shading budget per element class.
- Smoke wisps are barely legible against white — add slight gray value for video.
- Letter in post; the stacked rhyme column is too long for in-render text fidelity.
