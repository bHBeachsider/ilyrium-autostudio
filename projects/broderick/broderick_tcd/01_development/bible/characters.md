# Characters — The Cruuuuzing Dood

## cruuuuzing_dood
- **Key:** `cruuuuzing_dood`
- **Physical:** Man in his thirties; doughy face with a wide jaw; spiky unkempt black hair; heavy-lidded, half-closed eyes; broad grin of stained, gold-tinged teeth.
- **Wardrobe:** Red polo shirt; lit cigarette habitually held between his fingers on the steering wheel.
- **Affect:** Supremely mellow, self-satisfied, increasingly intoxicated; narrates his own cool in lazy sing-song rhymes until he is too high to function.
- **Role:** Sole character. A stoned driver who rap-chants nonsense rhymes about himself while cruising, announces "I'M HIIIIIIIGHH," and drives his blue sedan straight off a cliff.
