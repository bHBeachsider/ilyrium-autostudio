# Pilot Treatment — The Cruuuuzing Dood

**Logline:** A supremely mellow stoner rap-chants an escalating string of nonsense rhymes about his own coolness while driving, gets too high to function, and serenely pilots his blue sedan straight off a cliff.

**Treatment:** A slanted poster title floats over a tiny boxy blue sedan, and then we're riding shotgun: a doughy, spiky-haired man in a red polo, eyes at half-mast, cigarette dangling over the wheel, gold-stained grin wide, chanting his autobiography in lazy sing-song — "I'm a cruuuuzing doood, in a mellow mood" — each rhyme dumber than the last, from chewed meat to stewed prunes to a lawyer who sued. The flow dissolves; his head ghosts into double vision through the thickening smoke and he announces, beatifically, "I'M HIIIIIIIGH..." Smash cut to a wide shot: the sedan sails off a craggy cliff in total silence, debris scattering, green forest canopy waiting below. END, in white italics, before it lands.
