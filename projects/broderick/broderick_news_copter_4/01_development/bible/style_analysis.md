# Style Analysis — News Copter 4 on the Scene (SEED v0, 2026-06-11)

Panels examined: 01 (title card), 04 (skyline flyover), 06 (window scene — dialogue-heavy), 07 (apology end card). 4 of 7 panels.

## Medium Determination
**Hand-drawn digital illustration with digital production finishing, grayscale + one red accent.** Evidence:
- Underlying drawing is hand-made: anchor's hands/pen (panel 07) and helicopter skids show human line variation; the desk doodle gag is hand-scribbled.
- But production is tighter than sibling Broderick strips: rotor blades use a digital motion-smear/blur effect; skyline is stepped flat-gray silhouette layers (fill tool, not wash); balloon borders are clean.
- Lettering is a consistent bold-italic comic typeface (uniform glyphs across all panels) — typeset, not hand-lettered. Title card mixes display comic caps with a set red banner strip.
- The warning banner red is the only color in the strip — flat fill, no modeling. This contradicts the prior kernel's "flat color fills" as a general claim; color here is a single deliberate accent.

## Line / Color / Shading / Lettering
- **Line:** Even-weight confident digital ink; cleaner and less scratchy than Block Party / Slow on the Uptake. Minimal hatching.
- **Color:** Grayscale plus one flat brick-red (#B5391F est.) banner on the title card only.
- **Shading:** Flat tonal planes — fuselage one gray, skyline two stepped grays, window scene built from 3-4 gray values with the woman as the darkest silhouette mass. No gradients except rotor smear.
- **Lettering:** White rectangular balloons and caption boxes with black borders and straight tails; bold-italic comic typeface throughout; long apology text set as a free-standing right-column block (panel 07); END as white type on an angled black slab.

## Composition & Framing
- Title card: centered side-profile helicopter between stacked display type, banner along bottom edge.
- Broadcast scenes: rigid straight-on TV framing — anchor centered, inset graphic left, bug top-right; the immobility of the framing IS the joke.
- Flyover: lettering occupies the top third of white sky; helicopter mid-frame; skyline strip along the bottom.
- Window scene: voyeuristic straight-on through-the-glass framing, balloons flanking the window from inside and outside; the camera position implicates the viewer.
- Long-copy panels split frame: art left, text column right.

## Continuity Risks
1. Helicopter livery (numeral 4, tail lettering, registration code) must stay consistent across panels — generated text will garble; keep livery as a post overlay.
2. The anchor's smile must never waver across panels — identical pose/expression is the gag engine.
3. Silhouette discipline in the window scene: any added anatomical detail breaks both the style and content rating.
4. Red must not leak beyond the banner; any colorization of art panels is a style break.
5. Typeset balloons distinguish this strip from the hand-lettered Broderick strips — don't cross-contaminate lettering systems.

## Keep vs Fix (for regeneration)
**Keep:** flat layered-gray plane system; even ink line; bordered typeset balloons; rigid broadcast framing; silhouette nudity treatment; single red banner accent; rotor motion-smear; END slab badge.
**Fix:** station branding ("KOCK 4 NEWS", N-number) is parody-trademark territory — keep parodic but verify or regenerate marks; helicopter proportions drift slightly between title and flyover panels — lock one model sheet; ensure apology text block is typeset in post, never generated; the desk-doodle gag must be drawn deliberately, generators will sanitize or garble it.
