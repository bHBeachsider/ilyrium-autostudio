# Pilot Treatment — News Copter 4 on the Scene

**Logline:** A routine noon-news traffic toss goes off the rails when the helicopter pilot insults the commuters, spies on a woman through her apartment window, and berates her over the loudspeaker — forcing the still-smiling anchor to read an instant corporate apology.

**Treatment:** After a mock-bombastic title card — NEWS COPTER 4... ON YOUR SIDE! over a warning banner promising nudity and vulgar language — a square-jawed anchor with an unbreakable toothy smile tosses from the News 4 at Noon desk to the traffic chopper: "How's the expressway looking, Bruce?" Over a gray silhouetted skyline, Bruce delivers his report: same as always, buncha losers going to their shitty jobs — but something's heatin' up on the west side. The chopper swings to an apartment block and hovers as the copilot snickers that this is supposed to be traffic, not weather, and Bruce orders the camera closer. Through the glass: a woman caught mid-undress, reduced to an indignant silhouette, shouting that the helicopter is about to hit the building. Bruce's loudspeaker answer — "Shut up. News Copter 4 is on the scene." — is the franchise slogan weaponized. Hard cut: ONE MINUTE LATER. The anchor, smile untouched, pen idly clicking above a crude desk doodle, reads a flawless corporate disavowal of the disgraceful, totally unacceptable conduct of News Copter 4, in complete contradiction to the values we hold as a family. END. The comedy is the machine's instant, frictionless self-absolution.
