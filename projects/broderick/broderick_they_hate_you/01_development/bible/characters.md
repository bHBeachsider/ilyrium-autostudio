# Characters — They Hate You If You're Clever

## young_broderick
- **Key:** `young_broderick`
- **Physical:** Boy, about 6 years old (1st grade), slight build, shaggy mop of hair falling over his forehead, freckles, striped short-sleeve shirt, seated at a one-piece school desk.
- **Affect:** Absorbed, content and harmless while drawing; then hollow-eyed shock when his work is destroyed.
- **Role:** The narrator's childhood self. The strip is his autobiography ("Scene: 748-A") — the formative moment a teacher destroyed his drawing. The adult narrator voice in the caption boxes is the same character grown up.

## teacher
- **Key:** `teacher`
- **Physical:** Elderly schoolteacher, stout, hair in a severe tight bun, cat-eye glasses over heavy-lidded contemptuous eyes, deep frown lines and jowls, small drop earrings, dark striped sweater, carries a wooden pointer stick. First appears as a looming black silhouette.
- **Affect:** Cold, droning, authoritarian; speaks only in "BLAH BLAH BLAH" noise.
- **Role:** The antagonist. Catches the boy drawing, lectures him, then crumples his cat drawing in her fist and throws it away — the strip's titular proof that "they hate you if you're clever".

## classmate
- **Key:** `classmate`
- **Physical:** Skinny boy the same age, short hair, large wide eyes, gawky posture, seated at a desk in the background.
- **Affect:** Alarmed, wincing bystander.
- **Role:** Witness to the boy's humiliation in both classroom panels.
