# Pilot / episode treatment — They Hate You If You're Clever

**Logline:** In "Scene 748-A" of his sprawling autobiography, a man replays the
first-grade moment a teacher crumpled his cat drawing — the day he learned they hate
you if you're clever.

**Treatment:** A mock-grandiose title card — AUTOBIOGRAPHY, Scene: 748-A, over an
evolution silhouette march that ends in a slouched man chugging from a bottle — drops
us into a single childhood memory told in rough black-and-white sketch ink. A shaggy-
haired, freckled six-year-old hunches over his school desk, blissfully drawing a
cartoon cat, while the solid black silhouette of his teacher looms in the foreground
droning "BLAH BLAH BLAH" and a wide-eyed classmate watches in faded gray. The adult
narrator's deadpan captions carry the story: "My teacher caught me drawing in 1st
grade... she could have just said 'stop it'--". We hold on the teacher's jowled,
cat-eye-glassed scowl beneath the alphabet wall strip, then her arm thrusts into frame
and crushes the drawing in her fist as the boy recoils, hollow-eyed. The final caption
lands flat and unhealed — "...but she crumpled the shit up and threw it away!" — and
the END card slams down on a grievance still fresh 748 scenes later.
