# Characters — New York, New York

## ny_mascot
- **Age:** 40s-50s
- **Build:** Stout, barrel-chested
- **Hair:** Dark, scruffy under a dark baseball cap worn askew; thick mustache
- **Wardrobe:** Pinstriped baseball jersey (title card), rumpled shirt (closing card); always holding a drooping pizza slice and giving a giant thumbs-up
- **Affect:** Gap-toothed grin, brash unearned confidence
- **Role:** Bookend mascot personifying the city on the title and closing cards.

## shouting_man
- **Age:** 40s-50s
- **Build:** Broad, heavyset
- **Hair:** Wild, unkempt, spiky in every direction
- **Wardrobe:** Stained dark t-shirt, sweatpants; carries a worn bag and a plastic water bottle
- **Affect:** Blank and placid until he erupts; entirely unaware of his own supernatural volume
- **Role:** Protagonist of the anecdote — the homeless man who lives in Penn Station and detonates deafening nonsense bellows next to strangers.

## atm_businessman
- **Age:** 60s
- **Build:** Gaunt, stooped
- **Hair:** Balding, white side hair; wire glasses, hooked nose
- **Wardrobe:** Dark suit, white shirt, striped tie
- **Affect:** Prim concentration shattered into chest-clutching terror
- **Role:** First victim, blasted while using the ATM.

## phone_man
- **Age:** 30s-40s
- **Build:** Heavyset, round-shouldered
- **Hair:** Dark bowl haircut
- **Wardrobe:** Plain t-shirt, wristwatch
- **Affect:** Phone-absorbed obliviousness flipping to screaming panic
- **Role:** Second victim, loses his phone mid-blast.

## nypd_officer
- **Age:** 40s
- **Build:** Wiry
- **Hair:** Slicked dark hair, thick mustache, protruding teeth
- **Wardrobe:** Collared police uniform shirt with shoulder patches, dark tie
- **Affect:** Jaded, shrugging, crude
- **Role:** Officer at the NYPD information booth who explains they keep throwing the man out but he keeps coming back.

## bystander
- **Age:** Adult, indeterminate
- **Build:** Seen only as a dark head-and-shoulders silhouette
- **Wardrobe:** Indeterminate
- **Affect:** Alarmed (punctuated by an exclamation mark)
- **Role:** Concerned citizen who reports the distressed man to the police.
