# Characters — He Goes Like This

## dancer
- **Key:** `dancer`
- **Physical:** Lean man with a comically oversized head; slick dark side-parted hair, heavy brows, smug serene smile. Wardrobe: black turtleneck, light pleated trousers, flat dark shoes. Affect: total straight-faced commitment to every pose — cocky and showman-like — until the final panel, where his eyes go bloodshot red and stream red tears while the grin and the dancing continue.
- **Role:** Sole character. He demonstrates an escalating sequence of dance moves ("he goes like this... and he goes like that...") pose by pose, and the punchline is that beneath the routine he is silently weeping.
