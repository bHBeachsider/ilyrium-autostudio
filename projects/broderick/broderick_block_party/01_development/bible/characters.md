# Characters — Neighborhood Block Party

## dennis
- **Age:** 60s
- **Build:** Gaunt, wiry, hunched posture
- **Hair:** Short spiky gray hair
- **Wardrobe:** Dark short-sleeved camp shirt with open collar; shorts, tube socks, chunky sneakers (revealed in final panel); thick black rectangular glasses
- **Affect:** Permanent scowl, gritted teeth, deep frown lines; suspicious, blunt, socially abrasive; erupts into rage
- **Role:** Protagonist. The misanthropic neighbor who insults everyone at the block party and is finally undone by wasps stealing his frankfurter.

## dan_obrien
- **Age:** 50s
- **Build:** Heavyset, broad
- **Hair:** Short dark hair
- **Wardrobe:** Light polo shirt tucked into belted slacks; wristwatch
- **Affect:** Genial, eager to please; visibly crushed when rebuffed
- **Role:** Friendly neighbor on grill duty who greets Dennis and gets accused of causing food poisoning.

- **Role:** Beer-holding neighbor who tries to pull Dennis into a college basketball debate.
