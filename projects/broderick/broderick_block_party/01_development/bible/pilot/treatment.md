# Pilot Treatment — Neighborhood Block Party

**Logline:** A misanthropic retiree attends his neighborhood block party, insults every neighbor who dares to be friendly, and is finally destroyed by the only thing he came for — a frankfurter, stolen mid-tantrum by wasps.

**Treatment:** Under a cheerful suburban banner, Dennis — gaunt, scowling, sixty-something, armored in thick black glasses and a camp shirt — moves through a backyard block party like a slow toxin. Genial Dan O'Brien greets him from grill duty and is met with "DO I KNOW YOU??" and a graphic accusation of food poisoning; two beer-raising sports bros try to pull him into a UConn debate and are dismissed as primates babbling bonding gibberish. Dennis wants exactly one thing: a hot dog. He gets it — just as two neighbors small-talk about French drains over his head and wasps begin circling his food in lazy dotted loops. The banality and the menace tighten together until Dennis erupts in a full-volume obscene scream, swinging his frankfurter overhead at the swarm while the party freezes in horror. The dog slips loose, splats on the lawn, and Dennis hunches over it, spent and defeated, as a bearded onlooker bends fully forward to gawk at him upside-down between his own legs. THE END. Played entirely deadpan in grayscale; the comedy is the silence after each cruelty and the cosmic justice of the wasps.
