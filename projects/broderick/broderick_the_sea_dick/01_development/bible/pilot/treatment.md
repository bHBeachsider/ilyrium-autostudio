# Pilot Treatment — The Sea Dick (Part One)

**Logline:** A mythical sea pest who never hurts anyone — he just dicks people over and signs his work — becomes the subject of a deadly serious academic lecture that ends in a call for vivisection.

## Treatment
A mock nature-documentary short in three movements. First, the open sea: two wealthy bros toast an incoming jet-ski party on a gleaming custom powerboat until a scraping noise sends them to the rail — long gouges rake the hull, and a pale, pointy-eared creature surfaces in a ring of ripples, throws up both middle fingers, and announces "BARNACLES ALL OVER THE BOTTOM OF YOUR SHIP! COURTESY OF THE SEA DICK!" Second, escalation by montage: days later a giddy sport fisherman fights the catch of his life until a hand bursts from the waves holding scissors, snips the line, and sends him flying — the Sea Dick crows his catchphrase again. Third, the institution responds: at the prestigious Marine Research Academy, a turtlenecked professor solemnly briefs a darkened hall while the viral double-bird video loops behind him. The silhouetted audience derails the Q&A with theories, fourth-wall corrections, and a running riff about who the creature resembles — until the professor booms a wildly premature consensus: capture and vivisect the Sea Dick. The crowd recoils, one attendee keeps litigating his own tangent, and a white box stamps END OF PART ONE on the cliffhanger.
