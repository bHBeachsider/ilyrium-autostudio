# Characters — The Sea Dick (Part One)

## sea_dick
- **Key:** `sea_dick`
- **Age:** reads as a middle-aged man
- **Build:** lean humanoid torso, surfaces chest-high from the water
- **Hair:** pale, wet, slicked back
- **Features:** pointed ears, squinting gleeful grin, barnacles and scale patches scattered across bare chest and shoulders
- **Wardrobe:** none visible — sea creature
- **Affect:** gleeful, petty, self-promoting troll; signature move is raising both middle fingers and announcing his own name
- **Role:** title creature. A mythical sea pest who never hurts anyone — he barnacles hulls, cuts fishing lines, and 'dicks people over,' always signing his work 'COURTESY OF THE SEA DICK!' In-strip, audiences note he resembles Eric Trump.

## cap_bro
- **Key:** `cap_bro`
- **Age:** 30s
- **Build:** lanky, long-necked
- **Hair:** hidden under a backwards white baseball cap
- **Wardrobe:** tank top, gold chain, drink in hand
- **Affect:** loud, braying party hype-man ('PAWTY!')
- **Role:** wealthy boat bro whose custom powerboat is the Sea Dick's first victim.

## spike_bro
- **Key:** `spike_bro`
- **Age:** 30s
- **Build:** thick-necked, muscular
- **Hair:** tall spiky black hair; heavy jaw, prominent front teeth
- **Wardrobe:** shirtless, board shorts
- **Affect:** dim, enthusiastic, easily confused
- **Role:** cap_bro's deck mate; first to hear the scraping noise and films the hull damage on his phone — presumably the source of the viral video.

## fisherman
- **Key:** `fisherman`
- **Age:** 40s
- **Build:** wiry
- **Hair:** short and neat
- **Wardrobe:** t-shirt and shorts
- **Affect:** giddy hobbyist bravado, instantly humiliated
- **Role:** sport fisherman on a luxury yacht whose trophy catch is scissored off the line by the Sea Dick — flung backwards when the line snaps.

## professor
- **Key:** `professor`
- **Age:** 50s
- **Build:** stocky, rounded shoulders
- **Hair:** bald on top; grey-flecked mustache; glasses
- **Wardrobe:** dark turtleneck sweater
- **Affect:** ponderous academic gravity tipping into sudden bloodthirsty zeal
- **Role:** Marine Research Academy lecturer who briefs the public on the creature, then declares — to everyone's horror — a consensus to capture and vivisect the Sea Dick.

## audience
- **Key:** `audience`
- **Age/Build:** assorted silhouetted attendees, seen as dark heads from behind
- **Wardrobe:** indistinct silhouettes
- **Affect:** opinionated, bickering, prone to tangents
- **Role:** lecture-hall crowd supplying theories, corrections, fourth-wall jokes, and the running 'he looks like Eric Trump' bit.
