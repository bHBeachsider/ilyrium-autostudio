# Characters — Pass It On

## prisoner_english
- **Age:** 20s–30s
- **Build:** Wiry, lean
- **Hair:** Spiky upswept hair; freckled cheeks
- **Wardrobe:** Drab POW uniform shirt with sergeant chevrons on the sleeve
- **Affect:** Urgent, conspiratorial, all business
- **Role:** Originator of the whispered escape plan; starts the chain.

## prisoner_french
- **Age:** 30s–40s
- **Build:** Lean
- **Hair:** Dark beret (panel 1), soft white cap (panel 2); narrow mustachioed face
- **Wardrobe:** Horizontally striped shirt, gray trousers
- **Affect:** Puzzled listener, then uncertain whisperer
- **Role:** Second link; passes the plan on in French, already drifting.

## prisoner_russian
- **Age:** 40s
- **Build:** Stocky
- **Hair:** Thick dark mustache; military cap with star badge (later a light flat cap)
- **Wardrobe:** Plain gray tunic / pale fatigues
- **Affect:** Frowning, gruff, confidently wrong
- **Role:** Third link; relays a degraded Russian version.

## prisoner_sikh
- **Age:** 30s–40s
- **Build:** Broad
- **Hair:** Full dark beard; dark turban
- **Wardrobe:** Gray work shirt / fatigues
- **Affect:** Blank confusion, then earnest urgency
- **Role:** Fourth link; passes on a Hindi version now about guards passing wind.

## prisoner_african
- **Age:** 30s
- **Build:** Lean
- **Hair:** Soft crumpled uniform cap; dark-skinned
- **Wardrobe:** Button-pocketed tunic, loose fatigues
- **Affect:** Furrowed concentration, brisk delivery
- **Role:** Fifth link; relays a Swahili version with total conviction.

## prisoner_chinese
- **Age:** 30s–40s
- **Build:** Medium
- **Hair:** Short dark hair, faint mustache
- **Wardrobe:** High-collared utility tunic with chest pockets
- **Affect:** Stone-faced, measured, solemn
- **Role:** Sixth link; whispers a Chinese version now about urine seeking guards.

## prisoner_mohawk
- **Age:** 20s
- **Build:** Gangly, pale
- **Hair:** Tall upswept fair tuft, near-mohawk
- **Wardrobe:** Rumpled pale uniform shirt, later baggy t-shirt and knee-length shorts
- **Affect:** Vacant, utterly sincere
- **Role:** Final prisoner link; delivers the fully gibberish message straight to the guard.

## german_guard
- **Age:** 30s–40s
- **Build:** Solid, upright
- **Hair:** Hidden under a steel helmet; round glasses
- **Wardrobe:** Camp guard uniform — steel helmet, sleeve chevron, ammunition pouches, slung rifle
- **Affect:** Rigid, then baffled alarm
- **Role:** Punchline recipient; the escape plan reaches the enemy as pure nonsense.
