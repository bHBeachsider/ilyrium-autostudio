# Pilot / episode treatment

**Logline:** In a POW camp, a precise whispered escape plan is passed prisoner to prisoner across seven languages until it reaches a German guard as pure gibberish.

**Treatment:** In a stark gray barracks, a wiry English sergeant cups his hand and whispers the plan — escape at 0600 by the secret tunnel entrance, pass it on. His French neighbor, question mark hovering, passes along something vaguer about leaving around six but staying on guard. The Russian relays a half-remembered order about looking for something when a guard departs. By the Sikh prisoner's Hindi version, the plan concerns six guards who want to pass wind; the Swahili and Chinese relays calcify the nonsense with ever-greater solemnity. Each handoff is the same seated whisper two-shot — cupped hand, baffled stare, floating question mark — a metronome of confident miscommunication. Finally a vacant, mohawked prisoner in shorts marches up to a helmeted German guard and earnestly whispers the final transmission: "I AM WOO-WOO SHIT DING DONGS, DADDY GRAPES." The guard stares ahead, bewildered. END. A deadpan telephone-game farce where the escape plan defects to the enemy as noise.
