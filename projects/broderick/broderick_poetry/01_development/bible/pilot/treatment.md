# Pilot / episode treatment — Poetry (ˈpoʊətri)

**Logline:** A deadpan illustrated "poem" recites twelve absurd title-card portraits —
from Nutty Putty to Chicken Parm — and dares the audience to find the rhyme.

**Treatment:** A rapid-fire anthology short built as a gallery walk through twelve
single-panel vignettes, each a loud flat-color portrait introduced by its own
hand-lettered title spoken as deadpan voiceover: a hand presents NUTTY PUTTY like a
museum piece; a scowling FUDDY DUDDY raises a bony finger; a beaming strongman spreads
his arms behind a SIX-FOOT SUB; two faces mash into a lurid sunset HANKY-PANKY; CAP'N
STANKY wheezes through gold and missing teeth; the PENCIL CLUB's sole member smirks
over his pencil; MELLOW YELLOW grins through tinted glasses in a purple bokeh haze; a
mournful head begs us to FIX THE CELLO; a striped WIGGLY ARM undulates skyward; a
purple-suited showman points out HERE & THERE; MR. DROZDOFF grins at a silhouetted
student who mutters the film's only line, "WOT DAT DAYAR"; and a hulking bald man
glowers at his CHICKEN PARM. No story, no resolution — just escalating absurd
specificity, cut to black on the twelfth portrait.
