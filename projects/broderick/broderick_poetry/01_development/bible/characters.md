# Characters — Poetry (ˈpoʊətri)

This strip is an anthology of twelve one-panel portrait vignettes, each pairing a rhyming or
absurd title with a single figure. No character recurs across panels; each appears exactly once.
Keys below are listed in panel order.

## fuddy_duddy
- **Key:** `fuddy_duddy`
- **Physical:** Old man, 70s, gaunt and deeply wrinkled, balding with wild grey side tufts, heavy eye bags, stubble, one gold tooth. Green cardigan over a yellow collared shirt.
- **Affect:** Sour, scolding; one finger raised in rebuke.
- **Role:** Embodiment of his title panel, "FUDDY DUDDY".

## sub_man
- **Key:** `sub_man`
- **Physical:** Muscular man, 30s, broad shoulders, short dark hair and mustache, dark blue tank top.
- **Affect:** Beaming salesman pride, arms spread wide.
- **Role:** Presents the enormous sandwich in "SIX-FOOT SUB".

## kissing_couple
- **Key:** `kissing_couple`
- **Physical:** Two adults in tight profile, exaggerated full red lips, noses mashed together; one short dark hair, one fuller hair.
- **Affect:** Overwrought lurid passion.
- **Role:** The sunset embrace in "HANKY-PANKY".

## capn_stanky
- **Key:** `capn_stanky`
- **Physical:** Weathered sailor, 60s, ruddy pockmarked bulbous nose, scraggly grey-brown beard, gold tooth and gaps, white captain's hat with anchor emblem, red shirt.
- **Affect:** Wheezing, rank, overly familiar jollity.
- **Role:** Title figure of "CAP'N STANKY".

## pencil_club_man
- **Key:** `pencil_club_man`
- **Physical:** Young man, 20s, neat dark swept hair, narrow sly eyes, small smirk, mauve shirt, holds a yellow pencil upright.
- **Affect:** Smug insider secrecy.
- **Role:** Sole visible member of the "PENCIL CLUB".

## mellow_yellow
- **Key:** `mellow_yellow`
- **Physical:** Languid figure of indeterminate age, straight blond bowl cut, round yellow-green tinted glasses, heavy-lidded eyes, bucked front teeth, bulbous nose.
- **Affect:** Dazed narcotic serenity.
- **Role:** Title figure of "MELLOW YELLOW".

## cello_man
- **Key:** `cello_man`
- **Physical:** Heavy-jowled bald man, 50s, shown as a disembodied frowning head beside a cello with a snapped neck.
- **Affect:** Mournful pleading despair.
- **Role:** The aggrieved owner in "FIX THE CELLO".

## wiggly_arm_man
- **Key:** `wiggly_arm_man`
- **Physical:** Thin pale man, 30s, sparse short hair, weary half-lidded eyes, red-and-white striped shirt, green polka-dot pants; right arm impossibly long and rubbery, undulating in S-curves.
- **Affect:** Listless whimsy.
- **Role:** Title figure of "WIGGLY ARM".

## here_and_there_man
- **Key:** `here_and_there_man`
- **Physical:** Showman, 30s, curly brown hair, broad toothy grin, shiny purple suit with wide lapels over a white shirt.
- **Affect:** Game-show razzle-dazzle.
- **Role:** Points out "HERE & THERE".

## mr_drozdoff
- **Key:** `mr_drozdoff`
- **Physical:** Rumpled teacher, 60s, grey frizzy hair, deeply creased face, wide eager grin, bright green jacket over grey shirt, stands at a chalkboard bearing his name.
- **Affect:** Oblivious enthusiasm.
- **Role:** The teacher in the classroom panel.

## student_silhouette
- **Key:** `student_silhouette`
- **Physical:** Solid black silhouette of a student's head and shoulders, seen from behind facing Mr. Drozdoff.
- **Affect:** Slack-jawed incomprehension.
- **Role:** Mutters "WOT DAT DAYAR" — the only spoken line in the strip.

## chicken_parm_man
- **Key:** `chicken_parm_man`
- **Physical:** Hulking man, 50s, completely bald, enormous head with heavy drooping jowls, tiny scowling mouth, dark navy sweater.
- **Affect:** Seething dinner-table disappointment.
- **Role:** Glowers at his plate in "CHICKEN PARM".

Note: panel 1 ("NUTTY PUTTY") contains only an anonymous hand holding putty — no character key assigned.
