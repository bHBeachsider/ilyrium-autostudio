## House style for these numbers

- Stage directions in [brackets]; sung text plain.
- **No character ever hears the joke.** v11 §I: no irony anywhere in the frame.
- The satire lives in register, never in content. Contemporary institutional-progressive English used
  precisely and sincerely, never parodied or exaggerated — the more accurate the diction, the worse the
  effect.
- Nobody raises their voice. The piece has no shouting in it.
