# CASTING SLATE — NEW HARNOMY, U.S.A.

*Step 3.2. Six candidates per principal, 24 total. Copy any block into Higgsfield / ComfyUI / fal. Lighting is constant within a character — the candidates differ by face.*

**Framing (identical for all 24):** medium close-up from the chest, straight-on to camera, plain neutral background, even exposure, the face at rest and not performing, no smile, no eyebrow lift, no dramatic gesture

**Aspect:** 3:2  ·  **Negatives:** identical for all 24, see the JSON.

## How to judge
- Compare within a character only. The six differ by face, not by light.
- Read each character's test_question below before looking.
- Reject anything resembling a real person (step 3.3) BEFORE choosing.
- After picking four, run the silhouette test: all four at 40px, value only.
- Summer's pick must also pass the portrait test before it locks.

## 1. VALENCIA

*Mezzo. The Greek chorus. The only character who tells the truth.*

**Ask:** Do you believe she wants a manicure she cannot afford? The gap between what she is and what she wants to be IS the character.

**Trap:** Two opposite failures. Cast her already beautiful and the wanting evaporates. Cast her as picturesque poverty and she becomes the working-class cosplay the opera is attacking. She is neither: she is a young woman with real calluses who would fix them tomorrow if she had the money.
