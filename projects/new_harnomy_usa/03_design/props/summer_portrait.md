# Prop macro — Summer's Socialist-Realist portrait (HERO PROP, Act III + seeded earlier)

> Source: v11 §X (The Speech; The Arrest; Summer Beneath Her Portrait; Beats 1, 2, 4, 6), §XI (Design Coherence).

**HERO PROP:** a massive Socialist-Realist-inspired portrait of Summer Winters — "heroic, idealized, bathed in
golden light… the embodiment of revolutionary care, a mother of the movement despite her barrenness, gazing
toward an undefined but glorious future." Rises behind Pengold during the keynote; blazes above the Coronation.

**MATERIAL / RENDER:** painted-poster idiom *inside* a photographic frame — i.e. the *portrait* is a painting
(heroic Socialist-Realist style, golden light, upward gaze), but the *shot* is photographic realism (kernel).
Lit from below in Socialist-Realist gold. Scale: dominates the stage picture; "one rises as the other falls."

**IDENTITY LOCK:** the painted face must be *recognizably Summer* — generate the portrait FROM Summer's locked
anchor (`03_design/characters/anchors/summer.png`; Higgsfield `--soul-id summer` or image reference) with a
style transfer to Socialist-Realist painting; keep the anchor's face geometry. This is the single most
continuity-sensitive prop in the piece.
