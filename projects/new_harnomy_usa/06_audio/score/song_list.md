## Musical language (v11 §VII — binding for the Music Supervisor)
- Inverts model-opera convention: sophisticated contemporary techniques presenting progressive content whose
  contradictions become increasingly apparent; "earnest and slightly off — beautiful surfaces that don't quite
  resolve."
- Idioms per principal: Summer — soaring idealism; Pengold — measured authority; Dick — revolutionary fervor;
  **Valencia** — direct, economically precise; blues / work song / the musical language of actual labor, contemporary.
- **The Cellist's Fragment**: a short phrase in Valencia's modal idiom, seeded pre-show by a cellist "warming up"
  in the pit; returns only in her final aria; no character ever hears the cellist.
- **The Unresolved Chord**: not a triad; contains the TWEEEDS theme's triumphant major and Valencia's modal minor
  simultaneously; the ear cannot decide the key; grows "until it is almost unbearable"; hard cut, not a fade;
  eight seconds of silence.
- Pre-show: the fragment. Curtain call: none (see `../../01_development/bible/PRODUCTION_BLUEPRINT.md`).
