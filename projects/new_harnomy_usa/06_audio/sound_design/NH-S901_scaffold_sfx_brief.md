# NH-S901 — sound design brief

The teaser is **wordless and unscored**. Sound is not accompaniment here; it is the entire
soundtrack, and it carries the first five seconds alone over a black frame.

## The spine

One hammer, struck on a nail, in a large cold outdoor reverb — a square with hard buildings
on four sides, in winter, when sound carries. Unhurried. The rhythm of a man who has done this
before and is not being watched.

**The same recorded strikes open the teaser (SH001, over black) and return, quieter and further
off, under the empty dusk frame (SH010).** The last thing the audience hears is the first thing,
finished. Cut those two from one take so the room matches exactly.

## Layers

| Layer | Content | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Percussive | Hammer on nail, single unhurried strikes; nail-gun bursts; timber dropped on timber; the flat slap of a plank set down | SH001, 003, 007, 008 |
| Tonal | Chop-saw whine rising then cut off hard; circular saw; a cordless drill winding down | SH004 (foreground), 003/008 (distant) |
| Human | Workmen's voices at distance — cadence and tone audible, **words never intelligible**. A short laugh. No dialogue, ever. | SH003, 007, 009 |
| Ambient | Winter wind; a flag halyard ticking against a metal pole; distant traffic; one bus air-brake far off | throughout, rising in SH010 |
| Interior | SH005/SH006 are inside Pengold's office: the square's sound drops to a muffled remove behind glass, close room tone, a radiator tick. The saws are still there, just no longer his problem. | SH005, SH006 |
| Silence | SH011 thins to wind and halyard alone, then **one full second of nothing** before out. | SH011 |

## The one rule

**No music.** No score, no drone, no rising synth bed, no cello. The opera has not begun. The
first thing it will do when it begins is put people on that platform, and the teaser must end
before any of that is available to the audience.

A tonal bed under the build would tell the viewer how to feel about it, which is the wink this
teaser was explicitly built to avoid (Brad, 2026-09-04: structural rhyme only).

## Mix note

The interior cut at SH005 should be a real acoustic drop, not a fade — glass between us and the
work, sudden and slightly airless. When SH007 returns outside, the crossbeam seating should be
the loudest single event in the teaser.
