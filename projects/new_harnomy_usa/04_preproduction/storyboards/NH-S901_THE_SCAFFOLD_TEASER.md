# NH-S901 — "The Scaffold"
### 60-second teaser · wordless · no music · sound design only

> Concept: Brad, 2026-09-04. *The dais for the Anti-Thanksgiving rally goes up in the square
> outside city hall. It reads as a scaffold. Pengold watches from the window above. Hammering
> and saws.*

---

## The argument

The opera's governing law is stated in `world/setting.md`: **"The audience sees what the
characters cannot."** It is the same joke as the misspelled town — *harm* inside *harmony*,
which no character ever notices. This teaser is that law compressed into sixty seconds and
carried entirely by carpentry.

A municipal dais and a gallows are built from the same timber, in the same order, by the same
trade. Uprights, crossbeam, platform, steps. New Harnomy is building a stage. The frame it
raises is a scaffold. Nobody in the square sees it; the camera never says it.

Dick's end — *"led away in chains, literally shackled"* (motifs §4), the news of his death
arriving inside Valencia's aria — is not shown, not named, not hinted at in a title card.
It is simply **pre-built, in public, by cheerful people, four days early.**

## The two rules this teaser is built on

**1. Structural rhyme only — no wink.** Brad's call, 2026-09-04. Every shape a gallows has is
photographed honestly: the uprights going up, the crossbeam seated, the platform decked, the
steps assembled last. No rope. No noose. No cable loop at neck height. No shot that tips it.
`THE_CASTE.md` and v11 §I forbid the wink outright, and the wink would also be *cheaper* —
a viewer who spots it feels clever, and a viewer who spots it a beat too late feels the floor
go out. We want the second.

**2. Wordless.** No dialogue, no voiceover, no singing, no score. Sound design only: hammering,
a circular saw, a nail gun, timber dropped on timber, a chop saw's rising whine, workmen's
voices too far off to make out words, wind, a flag halyard, distant traffic. The absence of
music is the point — the opera has not started yet. When it does, the first thing it will do
is put people on that platform.

## What is deliberately NOT in this teaser

- **No Dick.** Brad's 60-second option excludes him. His absence is the whole construction: the
  scaffold is for someone, and the teaser never says who. Showing him would answer a question the
  audience has not yet learned to ask.
- **No Summer, no Valencia, no chorus, no portrait.** One face, once.
- **No text of any kind** — no title, no date, no logo, no chyron. Kernel negatives already forbid
  on-screen text; here it is also dramaturgy. The piece is not announced. It is *assembled.*
- **No music.** See rule 2.
- **No blood, no rope, no shackles, no anticipatory imagery.** The horror is that it is a perfectly
  ordinary Tuesday.

## Look

Full style kernel, unmodified: prestige political drama, House of Cards season-one grade, Deakins
single-source lighting, anamorphic, Alexa, 35mm grain, desaturated 12%, warm tungstens lifted, deep
browns and charcoals, cool blue only in the deepest blacks. Winter (motifs §7): short days, low sun,
bare trees, breath visible, frost.

The square must be photographed as a real municipal square in a real mid-sized American city —
`setting.md`: *"Not a cartoon, not a dystopia, not a stylized satire set."* The absurdity is entirely
in the people, and there are almost no people in this teaser. So there is no absurdity in it at all.
That is why it works.

## Sound design brief

| Layer | Content |
|---|---|
| Percussive | Hammer on nail (single strikes, unhurried, large cold reverb); nail gun bursts; timber dropped on timber |
| Tonal | Chop saw rising whine, cut off hard; circular saw; a drill |
| Human | Workmen's voices at distance — cadence audible, words never intelligible. No dialogue. |
| Ambient | Winter wind; a flag halyard ticking on a metal pole; distant traffic; a bus air-brake far off |
| Negative space | Shot 11 is near-silence. The teaser ends with one second of nothing. |

The hammer strikes in shot 1 should be the same recorded strikes that recur, quieter, under shot 10 —
so the last thing the audience hears is the first thing, now finished.

## Production notes

- **Song ID `NH-S901`.** The `NH-S9nn` marketing block was claimed in `SONG_ID_REGISTRY.json` on
  2026-09-04 so trailers can never collide with an act number (the failure class of NH-USA-003).
- **Only one character LoRA is needed:** `pengold_heavens_nh`. Per the library note, name the trigger
  *and* describe him — the bare Flux token does not bind.
- **Shots 5 and 6 are the only ones with a human face.** Everything else is timber, tools, hands,
  boots and weather, which is where generative video is strongest and identity drift cannot hurt us.
- Continuity: shots 2 → 3 → 7 → 9 → 10 are the same camera position on the square at five stages of
  build. Lock that framing once and reuse it; the build should read as one object growing, not five
  different structures.
