# Production Blueprint — the load-bearing elements outside the libretto

> Source: v11 §XI (Production Blueprint), §X (The Six Beats). "These are not atmospheric flourishes. They
> are load-bearing. Without them, the opera can still be performed; it cannot be experienced as intended."
> For a filmed/AI-rendered production, these translate into the opening sequence, the closing sequence, and
> the framing device (lobby/house). The two rendered bookends are specified shot-by-shot in
> `../../04_preproduction/storyboards/`.

## 1. Pre-Show Immersion (the audience is inside New Harnomy before the first note)

The lobby is decorated as the genuine world premiere of a sincere revolutionary opera: LYSTERIA Coalition
information tables; Man Bun Collective QR codes for donations; a PILF volunteer with a petition; the program
book printed in mauve, titled *A Framework for Progressive Awakening: Commemorative Edition* with a folded
performance insert; the house manager: "please silence your phones in solidarity with our most vulnerable";
a cellist warming up in the pit plays a fragment the audience will not recognize until Valencia's final aria;
ushers in professional-class progressive casual (Veja sneakers, independent-bookstore totes); natural wine at
the bar. Not one sign breaks character. Thesis: **"You did not come to watch. You came to attend."**

*Filmed equivalent:* a cold-open lobby sequence / title-free front matter in the same register; or the
framing content of a screening/premiere. Optional for the first render; mandatory for the full piece.

## 2. The First 90 Seconds (opening) → shot list `NH-S101_OPENING_FIRST_90_SECONDS.md`

1. Houselights stay up ten seconds longer than they should; people look around.
2. Hard cut to black — no dimmer — three full seconds of nothing. A cough sounds like a gunshot.
3. A single cold wash comes up on the **proscenium**, lit from the house side, so the audience sees the
   ornament of the theater itself. Held four seconds.
4. The stage is revealed: one room. Pengold's mayoral office at dawn (modest mid-century municipal;
   mahogany-laminate authority; desk; lamp still on; half-empty coffee cup; folded newspaper; three mauve
   books stacked on the credenza). Pengold already seated; does not react to being seen; he is reading.
5. **Twenty-two seconds** of him actually reading, silently, page turn included. "Long enough for a laugh
   to flicker when nothing is said. Long enough for the laugh to die in confusion. Long enough for discomfort
   to set in."
6. He sets the paper down carefully, aligned to the edge of the desk. Looks at the audience — not at us,
   past us, at the middle distance. Inhales to speak. Holds. Exhales without having spoken.
7. First bar of the orchestra: a single bass note. Low. Held.
8. Over that note, Pengold's recitative — to the federal investigation he can feel circling:
   *"Who among them / can I afford to lose?"*
9. Blackout on him. Houselights up on the **audience** as the chorus streams in from the vomitoria, already
   singing "Competing Anthems of Victimhood." The house has been claimed. The audience is inside New Harnomy.

## 3. The Curtain Call Problem (ending frame)

After the eight-second blackout: house lights to full — not stage lights. Stage remains dark. No curtain call.
The actors do not appear. Only the audience, looking at each other, the chord still ringing. After thirty
seconds — some standing, some clapping uncertainly — the cast walks out in street clothes from the wings and
takes seats in the front row, facing the empty stage. They sit. Then a single bow: Valencia, alone, from
her seat, without rising — a small nod to the house. The company never bows; they leave in ones and twos
through the audience. The audience leaves on its own timing, past the still-staffed LYSTERIA table, past the
PILF petition volunteer who asks if they'd like to sign on the way out. "Some people will sign. That is the
point." The production's final gesture: refuse the applause it has earned; send the audience home implicated.

*Filmed equivalent:* no end-title music; hold black; if credits, silent, late, and plain.

## 4. Design Coherence (every department holds the Wildean line)

- The mauve recurs in the lobby carpet, the ushers' lanyards, the program — never called out, never winked.
- Summer's portrait is glimpsed smaller, earlier: LYSTERIA lobby-table poster; background of a Pengold
  office scene; partially obscured at the festival. Its final ascension "must feel like something the audience
  has been staring at without seeing for three hours."
- Costume: no character is ever costumed as a joke (Dick's bespoke revolutionary; Summer's professional-class
  activist tailoring; Pengold's mayoral gravitas — all rendered with world-premiere seriousness). Valencia is
  the exception that proves the rule: real, practical, worn clothes.

## 5. The Load-Bearing Silences (never cut)

| Silence | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **The Twenty-Two Seconds** | Opening — Pengold reading | Establishes that the evening will not flatter the audience with entertainment. |
| **The Clarinet Line** | Finale beat 2 — Dick's "Pengold — you know me" over a single clarinet holding the TWEEEDS melody | "Not silence acoustically; silence dramatically. The stage listens to nothing. The fist closes here." |
| **The Eight Seconds of Blackout** | After the severed chord | "Where the opera deposits itself in the audience... Shortening it is not a technical compromise; it is an evisceration." |
