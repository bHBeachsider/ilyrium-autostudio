# "A Framework for Progressive Awakening" — the Bildersatz Framework

> Source: v11 §II. The single fictional ur-text that functions as the community's shared scripture — a
> pastiche of every post-modern progressive cliché compressed into one revolutionary document. This file is
> the canonical reference for any line, prop, or lyric that quotes it.

**Citation form:** characters call it "the Framework" or "the Bildersatz Framework"; formal citation is
**"Bildersatz, E.E."** — "with the bureaucratic efficiency of unexamined authority." Authorship is never
discussed. Bildersatz as a person: kernel casting canon `bildersatz` — "European, late 40s to early 50s;
1990s continental theorist; single authorized 1996 portrait" (an image that may appear on a poster/frontispiece;
never a speaking role in v11).

**Object:** all editions, regardless of publisher, are **mauve** — kernel spec: the brownish-purple of an
aged 1970s university-press paperback, never lavender / aubergine / pink. Prop macro:
`../../03_design/props/mauve_framework_book.md`. On stage in every location (see `world/motifs.md`).

## The Ten Principles (canon — quote exactly)

| # | Principle | Gloss (v11) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "The people do not yet know what they need" | The vanguard must guide those not yet conscious of their own liberation. Resistance to guidance is resistance to one's own freedom. *(Pengold's "They, the People" made flesh.)* |
| 2 | "Harm is structure, not intent" | Individual motivation is irrelevant. The system produces harm regardless of personal belief. *(Summer's defense of the Man Bun Collective.)* |
| 3 | "Silence is complicity" | There is no neutral position. Passivity is a choice with consequences. *(Invoked by Dick against Philip — and, unheard by anyone on stage, the verdict on Summer.)* |
| 4 | "The framework reveals truth" | Those who do not see what the Framework reveals require consciousness-raising. *(Pengold to journalists.)* |
| 5 | "Process IS praxis" | The work of having meetings about the work IS the work. *(Summer and Dick when challenged on lack of services/achievements.)* |
| 6 | "Discomfort signals growth" | If engagement with the Framework feels bad, it is working. |
| 7 | "Individual conscience is false consciousness" | The voice in your head questioning the collective is the colonizer speaking through you. *(Dick's core; what Summer trades away in the Coronation.)* |
| 8 | "We are building the future" | The destination is glorious though undefined. Trust the Framework. |
| 9 | "Those who question the path oppose the destination" | Critique of methods is opposition to goals. The Framework does not require defense. *(Pengold's immunization; Dick's campaign against Philip.)* |
| 10 | "The collective will emerges through correct consciousness" | Submit to discover freedom. The vanguard reveals the collective will. *(Dick: "Submit to discover freedom.")* |

## How each principal deploys it (feeds VOICE & SPEECH + dialogue)

- **Summer** — theoretical fluency; quotes constantly in grant applications, board presentations, media
  interviews; "writes as if Bildersatz were reviewing her grant proposals personally." Deploys #2 (biopower /
  harm is structure) and #5 (process is praxis) to defend the Man Bun Collective's lack of services.
- **Pengold** — strategic, not earnest; a pristine unread copy on his desk; deploys #9 against critics
  ("the very demand for 'balanced debate' is itself a form of repressive tolerance") and #4 against
  journalists ("you're operating within a discourse that naturalizes certain metrics of 'success'").
- **Dick** — genuine believer; deploys #7 and #10 constantly (warm embrace vs cold individualism); #5 when
  challenged on PILF's lack of achievements; "structural violence justifies counter-violence"; "decolonizing"
  everything including Thanksgiving and personal relationships. He never engaged Philip's argument — he
  treated the critique as heresy against #9.
- **Valencia** — the one who has not opened it (finale). Her arias translate it into economics.

## Prop / lyric rules
- Quote the Ten Principles verbatim; do not paraphrase them into new principles.
- Never show authorship discussion; never show anyone asking who wrote it.
- The books are never a sight gag. They are furniture, scripture, and (finale) a thump on the floor.
