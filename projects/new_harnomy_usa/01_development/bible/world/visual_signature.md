# Visual signature

> Kernel is fixed (`../../../style_kernel.json`); this file explains how the world wears it.

**Look (kernel, verbatim — reuse in every prompt):** prestige political drama, House of Cards season one
color grade, Roger Deakins single-source tungsten lighting, anamorphic cinematography, Arri Alexa, slight
35mm film grain, color palette desaturated twelve percent, warm tungstens lifted, deep browns and charcoals
in shadows, faint cool blue holding only in the deepest blacks, photographic realism, cinematic.

**Materials.** Mahogany laminate, municipal mid-century furniture, tungsten desk lamps, folded newsprint,
half-empty coffee cups, mauve paperbacks (matte, softened spines, highlighter bleed), heritage wool and
selvedge denim (Dick), tailored professional-class activist wear (Summer), mayoral suiting (Pengold),
worn practical clothes (Valencia), festival display tables, canvas totes, wire-frame glasses, natural-wine
bottles in the lobby.

**Light.** Single-source tungsten, motivated (desk lamp still on at dawn; podium light; Socialist-Realist
gold from below on the portrait; a single cold wash on the proscenium; Valencia's "own light" — one step
downstage into it). Deep shadow holds detail in brown/charcoal. Winter daylight, when it appears, is thin
and cool and stays out of the principals' faces.

**Color.** Desaturated by ~12%; warm tungstens lifted; the only saturated events are (a) the Socialist-Realist
gold of the portrait and (b) the mauve of the books — and mauve is deliberately un-saturated (brownish, aged).
Faint cool blue only in the deepest blacks. No contemporary saturation (kernel negative).

**Camera.** Anamorphic, Alexa, cinematic aspect (2.39:1 / 21:9 where the engine supports it, else 16:9);
locked-off and slow moves; long holds; cut on stillness. Faces are never played to camera.

**Documentary exception.** Valencia is "photographed truthfully in documentary register, outside the
production grade" (kernel casting canon): flatter, truer light; the world's grade lifts slightly when the
frame is hers. Do not over-do it — the audience must feel it, not see it.

**Two allowed spectacles.** The rising portrait (Socialist-Realist gold, heroic scale) and the finale's
locked stage picture. Everything else is restraint.

**Kernel negatives (never):** cartoon, anime, illustration, painted look, exaggerated lighting, lens flare,
smile at camera, comedic expression, on-screen text, chyron, caption overlay, contemporary saturation,
winking, irony, logo, watermark, modern smartphone, laptop screen prominent in frame.

**Engine tails.** Midjourney: `--style raw --stylize 200 --v 7`. Higgsfield: see kernel `engine_tails.higgsfield`
(Soul Cinematic stills 21:9 2k; Seedance 2.0 / Kling 3.0 i2v from the locked keyframe; genre `drama`).
