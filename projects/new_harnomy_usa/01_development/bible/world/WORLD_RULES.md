# World rules / logic

> What is and isn't possible in New Harnomy. Source: v11 §I–§III, §VIII–§IX.

1. **Realist world, no non-realist laws.** Contemporary American city; nothing supernatural. The only
   "unreality" is Valencia's dramaturgical status: she can address the audience and comment on the action;
   the principals cannot hear her arias. In the finale, Dick — for the only time — *sees* her (beat 3).
2. **The Wildean law is physics here.** No character is ever self-aware about the irony surrounding them.
   No winks. This is enforced at performance (register) and at QA (`acting_at_camera` fix).
3. **The Economic Silence is structural.** Summer, Pengold and Dick do not discuss money, class, labor,
   ownership, who pays or who works for whom. If a draft line has them doing so, the line is wrong.
   Only Valencia addresses material conditions.
4. **The Framework is scripture; authorship is never questioned.** "To ask who wrote it would reveal that
   you haven't done the work." Characters cite "Bildersatz, E.E." Ten Principles (see `../THE_FRAMEWORK.md`)
   are quoted to win arguments and establish credentials.
5. **The Selective Lens.** Principals know history — colonialism, the slave trade, the labor movement — but
   are willfully blind to leftist authoritarianism (USSR, Eastern Europe, Cuba, Cambodia, the Cultural
   Revolution). This is ideology, not ignorance.
6. **Names are true and unheard.** New Harnomy, LYSTERIA, PILF, TWEEEDS, GRIFT, Man Bun Collective, Summer
   Winters, Dick Suede, Valencia Strong, Pengold Heavens — self-revealing to the audience, invisible to the
   characters. No character ever remarks on a name.
7. **The mauve is universal.** Every Framework copy in every location is the same mauve. (Kernel spec.)
8. **Structural inversion of the model opera** (v11 §IX): imagined symbolic oppression not material;
   credentialed elites claim victimhood; enemies are abstractions or each other; "consciousness" is
   performed for career advancement; trials = being called out; strategy = grant-writing and networking;
   the decisive "battle" = the love triangle collapsing and patronage exposed; revolutionary order = rebranding
   the grift; cancelled friends briefly mourned then forgotten; no tonal resolution; winter not spring.
9. **Money is modest and real.** $5M matching → $50M federal; the GRIFT grant; a trust fund; PILF's missing
   money; rent due. Small stakes make the betrayal more devastating, not less.
10. **The cycle continues.** Nothing fundamental changes at the end: "Year-end becomes new year planning.
    Same grift, new season. New federal grants to chase. New sacrifices to be selected."
11. **Real public figures are never depicted.** Legal rule (kernel): specify every character by age, origin,
    build, wardrobe and affect only. v11 names some real politicians as *models* for Pengold's type; that
    is background for the writer, never a rendering instruction. Regenerate any render that resembles a real
    person.
12. **Ambiguity is preserved at the end.** The opera refuses to say whether Summer will speak. No draft
    resolves it.
