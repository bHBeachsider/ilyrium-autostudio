# Recurring motifs

> Source: v11 §II, §VI (Staging Note), §VII, §X, §XI (Design Coherence). Motifs become `set_dressing` /
> `prop_business` prompt slots and QA checks. The kernel enforces the mauve spec.

## 1. The mauve books (primary motif — every location, every act)

Copies of *A Framework for Progressive Awakening* — "the Framework" / "the Bildersatz Framework",
cited "Bildersatz, E.E." Visible in offices (lining shelves), on festival display tables, carried by
characters, gestured toward, held up during speeches, raised by the chorus in the finale "the way
congregants raise hymnals." All editions share the color.

**Kernel spec (fixed):** *"the brownish-purple of an aged 1970s university-press paperback, never lavender,
never aubergine, never pink."* v11: "a soft, cultivated purple-grey that suggests both sophistication and
accessibility." Reconcile as: dusty, brownish, warm, faded academic paperback — read the kernel spec into
every prompt; QA `failure_fixes.mauve_reads_lavender` applies.

Per-character book states (renderable):
- **Summer's copies** — multiple, prominently displayed, highlighted, annotated, underlined, cross-referenced.
- **Pengold's copy** — a single pristine copy on his desk, conspicuously unread, strategically positioned;
  three stacked on the credenza behind him at dawn.
- **PILF's copies** — multiple, highlighted and annotated in competitive colors by different staff members;
  genuinely read.
- **Valencia's copy** — the one she has NOT opened (finale beat 3), which she opens, closes, and lets fall
  (beat 5): "a single unamplified thump."
- **Chorus copies** — raised in unison (beat 1); frozen mid-gesture, still raised (beat 6).
- **The program book** — printed in mauve, titled *A Framework for Progressive Awakening: Commemorative
  Edition*, with a folded performance insert.

The mauve also recurs, un-called-out, in the lobby carpet, the ushers' lanyards, the program book.

Prop macro: `03_design/props/mauve_framework_book.md`.

## 2. Summer's Socialist-Realist portrait

"Heroic, idealized, bathed in golden light... the embodiment of revolutionary care, a mother of the
movement despite her barrenness, gazing toward an undefined but glorious future." Rises behind Pengold
during the keynote; at half-height and rising (beat 1); apex, held, blazing (beat 2); above the
Coronation (beat 4); "portrait blazing above them" in the locked stage picture (beat 6).
**Seeding rule:** it must already have been glimpsed, smaller, earlier — on a poster at the LYSTERIA lobby
table, in the background of one of Pengold's office scenes, partially obscured at the festival. "Its final
ascension must feel like something the audience has been staring at without seeing for three hours."
Prop macro: `03_design/props/summer_portrait.md`.

## 3. The hand on the shoulder (the counter-sacrament)

Pengold's right hand on Summer's left shoulder — the Mafia-Wife Coronation. "In a work where the mauve
Framework is the Eucharist, the touch is the confirmation." She does not move, does not look at him,
looks out past the house at nothing. Pengold's face: "not triumph, not smugness — settled... The smallest
possible smile. Held." — the casting test.

## 4. Chains

Dick "led away in chains — literally shackled." Model-opera trial imagery inverted.

## 5. The cellist's fragment / the unresolved chord (musical motifs)

A short phrase in Valencia's modal idiom seeded during the lobby hour (a cellist warming up), unheard by
any character, returning only in her final aria. The closing chord contains both the TWEEEDS theme's
triumphant major and Valencia's modal minor at once; not faded — severed. See `../../../06_audio/music/score_brief.md`.

## 6. Silence and stillness

The 22-second read; the clarinet line under Dick's plea; the eight-second blackout; the frozen chorus;
"nobody breathing." Cut on stillness. Performance register: no telegraphed emotion.

## 7. Winter / barrenness / scarcity

Thanksgiving week; Summer *Winters*; sterilization; the promise of abundance turned to barrenness.
Where model operas had spring and harvest, this has winter light and short days.

## 8. Wardrobe as ideology (see character bibles)

Dick's bespoke revolutionary (tweed-adjacent heritage fabrics — "the TWEEEDS connection he never makes");
Summer's professional-class activist tailoring; Pengold's mayoral gravitas; Valencia's real, worn clothes.
Ushers "in period: professional-class progressive casual, Veja sneakers, tote bags from the local
independent bookstore."

## 9. Acronyms that tell the truth no one hears

LYSTERIA, PILF, TWEEEDS, GRIFT, the Man Bun Collective — see `factions.md`. Never on screen as text
(kernel negatives: no on-screen text/chyron/caption/logo) unless the shot is *about* the object (a program
cover, a lanyard); prefer legibility through staging, not typography.
