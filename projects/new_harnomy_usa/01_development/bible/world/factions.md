## The systemic grift (v11 §IV)

PILF's mismanagement is endemic, not unique: grant funds allocated by whim; reporting "creative at best,
fraudulent at worst"; outcomes manufactured; metrics massaged; impact statements written before programs
launch; the system functions through patronage, not programs. Federal oversight means someone must take
the fall. Pengold's calculation (four goals in one move): placate oversight with a contained scandal;
protect the system with a scapegoat whose failure looks individual; remove a romantic rival; demonstrate
"commitment to accountability" while preserving the machine.
