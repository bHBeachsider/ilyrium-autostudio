# THE CASTE — how Summer, Pengold and Dick are photographed

*Canon 2026-09-02 (Brad). Binding on every prompt, keyframe and shot containing a principal.
Valencia is the exception and the point.*

---

## The rule

**Summer, Pengold and Dick are one class, not three individuals.**

They are children of privilege cosplaying socialists — *regardless of their actual backgrounds*. Polished,
scrubbed, gym-going. They look like actors and athletes. They are the products of elite academic institutions
and the social connections that come with them, and they wear masks they were trained to wear. They subscribe
to all the correct lines of thought and are oblivious to the absurd contradictions that define their
existences. They are the new masters of the universe, and the audience must see that on sight.

They ultimately hold the vulgar working class they profess to represent in contempt.

## Why this is a casting rule and not a note about three people

Cast them as three different kinds of person and the audience reads three individuals with individual flaws
— a story about some bad actors. Give them **one surface** — same schools, same gym, same dentist, same
certainty — and the audience reads a **system**. The satire becomes structural instead of personal, which is
what v11 is actually about: a class that has professionalised revolution.

It also makes Valencia's function legible in a single wide shot. Three glossy people and one real one, and
the audience knows which one to believe before anybody sings.

## The renderable block

Every principal prompt carries this, verbatim:

> the product of elite schools and a good gym - a lean maintained physique under the clothes, excellent
> upright posture, expensive orthodontics, clear well-rested skin with regular professional maintenance, a
> good haircut, groomed brows, no visible wear of any kind, no lines earned from worry; a completely
> untroubled face that has never been seriously contradicted and does not know that is unusual; the settled
> physical ease of someone who has never once worried about money

## ⚠️ CERTAIN, NOT SMUG — the line that protects the Wildean law

The temptation is to render smugness. **Do not.** v11 §I sets a strict creative law: the characters remain
completely earnest and oblivious, with no winks to the audience and no moments of self-awareness. **A smug
face is a wink** — it tells the audience how to feel and lets them off the hook.

What the entitlement actually looks like is **the total absence of doubt**. Serene, pleasant, well-rested,
frictionless, unbothered. A face that has never been seriously contradicted. Standing there holding a winning
lottery ticket in each hand — which is not a sneer, it is unearned security worn as ordinary comfort.

That reads as far more damning, because the audience has to supply the judgment themselves.

**The contempt lives in what they DO, never on the face.** Dick's shakedown of the bodega owner is where the
hatred is visible. Summer's silence is where it is visible. Pengold's sacrifice of Dick is where it is
visible. Never in an expression.

Prompt this positively: *"pleasant, serene and entirely certain, calm and unbothered, an open untroubled
expression, well-rested and comfortable."* Never "smug", "arrogant", "sneering" or "superior" — those words
produce a performed face.

## What this changes in the existing bibles

**Pengold.** His Northeast working-class origin is now **invisible on the surface**. The earlier direction —
"the polish sits uneasily, origin showing through, something slightly effortful" — is superseded. The mask
worked completely. Nobody can see the intern any more, *including him*, which is exactly v11's line that "he
cannot find the person who existed before the construction began." The construction is seamless. That is
more tragic, not less.

**Dick.** "Little Dickie" — the family called him soft — is now a fact about his **character, not his body**.
He is gym-built, tanned and immaculate. The softness moved from the physique to the eyes: nothing behind
them, no resilience, never had to be hard. A physically impressive man with nothing underneath is more
unsettling than a visibly weak one, and it makes his collapse land harder.

**Summer.** Largely unchanged, but the maintenance becomes explicit: early forties held to read younger,
discreetly and expensively kept.

## Valencia does NOT get this block

She never receives one word of it. No gym, no orthodontics, no salon, no ease. She is the only principal
photographed truthfully, and the gap between her surface and theirs is the opera's thesis worn on four
bodies. See her bible and `casting/valencia_v3/VAL-H01.png`.
