## 📝 CASTING REVIEW — 2026-09-02

**He is not a villain, he is the intern who watched.** v11: he sat front row, attended every office hour,
memorised his professors' work to quote back at them. The old descriptor said "calculating unhurried eyes,"
which was producing exactly the smooth-villain squint the trap warns against — my own prompt encoding the
failure it was written to prevent. Replaced with **eyes that have been listening a long time.** Attentive,
never scheming.

**Where the class tell actually shows.** He is Northeast working-class origin wearing acquired polish, and
that renders in three places, now all in the prompt: heavy hands that did not come from an office; grooming a
shade **too** precise, the overcorrection of a man who learned the rules rather than grew up inside them; and
tailoring that is correct but worn like it is new.

**The pairing to hold in mind:** Dick's expensive clothes perform poverty. Pengold's expensive clothes
perform belonging. Both are costumes; only Pengold knows it.

Also fixed: "no ostentation" was a negative instruction sitting inside a positive prompt, which image models
handle badly. Restated as "plain, correct, unshowy."

### What he became, and the note that did not survive casting

The direction on 2026-09-03 asked for a soft, slightly squishy man — a political
consultant who eats out at too many high-end restaurants — and explicitly not fat and
not rough. Four text candidates were generated across that range. **Brad chose the
leanest of them (PEN-T01).**

So the well-fed plushness is *not* in the anchor, and this bible describes the face
that exists rather than the one that was asked for. The tell moved: he is not soft, he
is **smooth**. No weathering, no hard-won lines, nothing that suggests he has ever
worked outdoors or been hit — an indoor animal with an expensive barber. The lean
saturnine face reads colder than the plusher candidates did, and that is consistent
with Brad rejecting the softest one (T03) as **too likeable**.

Two limits from the original note DO still hold and must not drift:

- **Never rough or coarse.** He is groomed, not weathered.
- **Bearded, not clean-shaven.** The hipster beard is precisely barbered and
  expensively maintained — a costume of authenticity worn by a man with none, which is
  `THE_CASTE.md` in a single detail.

This supersedes both the earlier direction to make him heavier and broader through the
shoulders, and the softness note above it.


Carries the caste block — see `../THE_CASTE.md`.

### The consultant read is the right one

He does not look like a mayor; he looks like the person who *makes* mayors. That is exactly right — v11's
Pengold learned power as a TWEEEDS intern by watching how funding decisions were made and who got rewarded,
and he has been operating that machinery ever since. The face should belong to the room where the deal
happens, not the podium.

**The mask is seamless.** Nothing of the Northeast working-class origin shows. He passed completely, and
nobody can see the intern any more — including him. That is v11's "he cannot find the person who existed
before the construction began," and it is more tragic than a visible seam would be.

**Open note:** the caste pass flattened his *build* slightly. He should remain the heavy one of the three
against Dick's lean — heaviness is character, not surface, and the caste rule governs surface only.
