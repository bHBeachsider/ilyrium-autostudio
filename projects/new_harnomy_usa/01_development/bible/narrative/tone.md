# Tone

> Source: v11 §I (Wildean Principle), §VII (Musical Language), §XI (Production Blueprint), and the Style Kernel.

**Register (kernel, fixed):** sincere, reverent, serious. No irony, no comedic timing, no winking.
The production "extends the Wildean principle to the audience itself. No irony anywhere in the frame."

**How the comedy works.** It never lives in the frame or the performance. It lives in the gap between
how the characters see themselves and what the audience sees. Every department plays it straight:
Dick's bespoke-revolutionary wardrobe, Summer's professional-class activist tailoring, Pengold's mayoral
gravitas "must be rendered with the same seriousness as any other world-premiere opera. The costume
closes that gap only by being completely sincere." No character is ever costumed as a joke.

**Emotional weather.** Winter, barrenness, scarcity (Summer *Winters*, Thanksgiving week) — where model
operas had spring/harvest/abundance. Genuine feeling expressed through frameworks that cannot contain it.
"Beautiful surfaces that don't quite resolve, harmonic progressions that promise satisfaction but deliver
something slightly different."

**Visual temperature (kernel look).** Prestige political drama; House-of-Cards-season-one grade; single-source
tungsten (Roger Deakins register); anamorphic; Alexa; slight 35mm grain; desaturated ~12%; warm tungstens
lifted; deep browns/charcoals in shadow; a faint cool blue only in the deepest blacks; photographic realism.

**Valencia is the exception that proves the rule.** She is "photographed truthfully in documentary
register, outside the production grade" (kernel casting canon) and dressed in "what actual working
people actually wear... clothes that have been worn more than once this week." She is the only character
who knows she's in a satire; the camera knows it too, and shows it only through her.

**Silence is a material.** Three load-bearing silences (22 seconds of reading; the clarinet line under
Dick's plea; the eight-second blackout). "The stage manager who cuts any of these to 'move the show'
has not understood the show."

**Failure modes to reject at QA (kernel `failure_fixes`).** Acting at camera (smile / eyebrow lift /
dramatic gesture); mauve reading lavender/aubergine/pink; any comedic expression; contemporary saturation;
lens flare; on-screen text.

**On whether the humor survives "no winks" — see `COMEDY_AND_TRAGEDY.md`** (2026-09-04). Short form: it is
a tragedy in which the audience discovers the comedy; the humor is unperformed, not absent. Comedy in the
recitative, tragedy in the arias; give the laugh a beat of silence; sharpen, never soften; never perform the
joke. The model is *Nixon in China*, not Sophocles.
