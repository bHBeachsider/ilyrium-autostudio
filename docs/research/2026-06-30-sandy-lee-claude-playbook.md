11. **AI thumbnails = packaging.** **Nano Banana Pro** (via Gemini): take an outlier thumbnail +

12. **Consolidated "content studio" app.** Everything in one place, built with Claude Code in

13. **Monetization & the 2026 algorithm.** "The purpose of creating content is to make money."

    client work. `[25:41][1:06:31][1:07:00]`
14. **Inbound/outbound via repurposing.** Paste one YouTube link → **6 LinkedIn angles**;
